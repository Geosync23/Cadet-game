Deck 2: Command & Science

Sketch 3, for build 0.6.6. Bow points forward, port side is on the left when facing it. Where the senior crew live and the ship does its science. Aft of the lift, two quiet carpeted corridors run to the stern, with suites along the hull on both sides and the Captain's quarters between them, across the whole stern. Forward, the Survey labs, the chart room and the comms room line a corridor that ends at a window onto the sensor array in the bow. The Watch office looks over the lift lobby. Tap any room. Open questions I had to guess at
  • No room behind a locked room. Every suite is one room with its own door onto a corridor, so nobody's bed sits behind someone else's locked door. Deck 4's cabin template can furnish the suites as bigger cabins (5 × 4, about 1.7× an officer's cabin). The Captain's quarters (11 × 6) will want their own layout: a dining table for twelve, a study and a sleeping alcove by the stern windows.
  • The wardroom became the Captain's quarters. Sketch 2's wardroom grew into the Captain's private quarters, and the Captain hosts senior dinners at her own table. The small senior pantry beside it stays open, as a shortcut between the corridors. Do we still want a shared senior lounge somewhere else?
  • Who lives where. Port: Commander Vance on the stern corner, the Works Chief, then a visitors' suite (new idea) by the lift. Starboard: Envoy Saruth on the stern corner, Chief Holt next door (a story hook, or a bad idea?), then the Infirmary Chief, who isn't named yet. Dr. Sato lives forward, by her labs, in an inner suite with no windows.
  • New public rooms. The chart room and the comms room are new ideas for the extra length. Are they the right rooms for Survey and the bridge's comms crew?
  • Width. At 18 tiles, Deck 2 is wider than Deck 5 (16). The doc only says the hull is narrow at the top and bottom, so I graded it: 10, 18, 22, 20, 16, 12.
  • The array. It's one big bow room for sensors and comms (tier 3), watched through glass at the end of the science corridor, as with Deck 5's fan hall, and reached through a shut door from the comms room.
  • Lab gas lines. These are a sealed gas plant beside the main lab. I guessed tier 2 (pipes and valves, like the manifold room).
  • Watch office. No holding cell is sketched. Does the Watch need one?
  • Access. I've assumed the lobby, all three corridors, the labs, the chart room, the comms room, the pantry, the Watch office and the washroom are open from the start, and every private quarter is locked.
