Open questions I had to guess at
  • Suites are one room each. Sketch 1 had a day room and a bedroom per suite, which left every bedroom shut behind a locked room. Now each suite is a single hull room (4–5 × 4, about 1.5× an officer's cabin) with its own door onto the corridor, so Deck 4's cabin template can furnish it as a bigger cabin.
  • The wardroom. This is a new idea, to fill the middle of the loop: a lounge where the senior crew take a quiet drink and host guests, with a pantry behind it. It also makes a shortcut between the port and starboard corridors. Keep it, or make the middle something else?
  • Who lives where. Port: the Captain on the stern corner, Commander Vance, then the Works Chief nearest the lift, for the early start down to Deck 5. Starboard: Envoy Saruth's cabin mirrors the Captain's as a courtesy, with Chief Holt next door (a story hook, or a bad idea?), then the Infirmary Chief, who isn't named yet. Dr. Sato lives forward, by her labs, in an inner suite with no windows.
  • Width. At 18 tiles, Deck 2 is wider than Deck 5 (16). The doc only says the hull is narrow at the top and bottom, so I graded it: 10, 18, 22, 20, 16, 12.
  • The array. It's one big bow room for sensors and comms (tier 3), watched through glass at the end of the science corridor, as with Deck 5's fan hall. Split comms off into a room of its own?
  • Lab gas lines. These are a sealed gas plant beside the main lab. I guessed tier 2 (pipes and valves, like the manifold room).
  • Watch office. No holding cell is sketched. Does the Watch need one?
  • Access. I've assumed the lobby, both corridors, both labs, the wardroom and pantry, the Watch office and the washroom are open from the start, and every suite is locked.
