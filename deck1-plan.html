Deck 1: Bridge

Sketch 1, for build 0.6.6. Bow points forward, port side is on the left when facing it. The smallest deck, at the very top of the hull. Step out of the lift and the bridge doors are right ahead, with the observation room behind you, looking aft over the whole ship. Few people come up here, so every visit feels like an occasion. Tap any room. Rooms ringed in gold are new ideas, not in the design doc yet.
Deck 1 at a glanceThe bridge fills the bow: a viewscreen across the bow wall, a sunken command ring with a curved rail behind it, and the rear stations along the aft wall. The Captain's ready room opens off the bridge. Aft of the lift, the observation room doubles as the conference room. The service layer is small: the relay room and a sub-floor crawl under the rear stations, plus a utility closet.20 × 10 tiles, the narrowest deck (Deck 3 is 22 wide). The lift lines up with Decks 3, 4 and 5 (game lift: [8, 5], car rows 3–5, ladder row 6, trunks row 2).
Open questions I had to guess at
  • Size. The design doc says about 14 × 10. This is 20 × 10: the bridge itself is about 10 × 10, and the lobby round the spine and the observation room take the rest. Trimming the observation room to 3 columns would get it to 18.
  • Sunken ring. The build has no floor steps, so the ring is shown by a curved rail (a bar curve) behind the chairs. A real sunken floor would need a step kit and a floor-painter change.
  • Viewscreen. It sits on the bow hull as a dress piece that follows the curve, like the music room's stage. A wall curve in front of the hull would leave tiles behind it that nobody can reach. There are no bridge windows; the view is in the observation room.
  • Getting in. Bridge and observation room doors are drawn as open. Should the lift panel or the bridge doors stay shut (with tap text) until a story event calls you up?
  • Floors. The bridge uses plate for now. It probably wants a floor of its own, such as dark carpet with an inlaid ring.
  • Bridge washroom. This is a new idea. Keep it, or let the watch ride down to Deck 2?
  • Top of the spine. I've assumed the ladder and trunks end here, at a trunk head behind glass.
