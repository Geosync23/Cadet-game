# 0.60.7 line keys (head writer 0607)

The names the engineers wire for 0.60.7's new lines. Every key below is a **kind** in a `.lines` file, so the game
reads it the usual way: `lineFor(who, kind, ctx)` for a crew member's line, or the text helper (like `careerText`) for
words the game shows that nobody says. Flags in `? flag:` conditions are set by your hook through `ctx.flags`, as
Zeke's tour does today.

Where the words go: `content/banks/talk.lines`, `hobby.lines`, `leisure.lines`, `bar.lines`, `clothes.lines`,
`stores.lines`, `crewjobs.lines`, `needs.lines`, `clockscreen.lines`, `calendar.lines`; Zeke's lines in `content/lines/torres-zeke.lines`; tradition words in `content/trad-words/<id>.words`.

**Texts** (shown by the game, said by nobody): no voice tokens; only the placeholders listed with the key.
**Card lines** 90 characters, **bubbles** 60 (as lines_check already says).

If a key here doesn't fit your code, tell the head writer (via the lead) and we'll rename it; don't rename on your side.

## Traditions engineer: the twelve new traditions, the Calendar

`== here`, `== thanks`, `== chatter`) so you can paste them into your `.trad` files; you own the fields. Proposed ids

`reading-corner`. Gym club keeps the id `running` with new words in `trad-words/running.words`.

`flag:trad.beam.chief`), in order. The postcard: `postcard.write`, `postcard.sent` (**texts**).

Calendar texts: `cal.count` ("{n} traditions today"), `cal.one`, `cal.none`, `cal.same` ("same time as {trad}"),
`cal.member` (Gym club: "You're a member"), `cal.title`.

`{max}` `{part}` `{shelf}` `{drink}`. Tips (`tip.*`) may run to 200 characters.
