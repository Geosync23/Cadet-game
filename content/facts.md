## 1. The ship and the world

- **The ship:** the **CSV Wren** ("the Wren"; crew say "she"), a 75-person survey ship of the **Concord Survey Service**,
  a peaceful exploration fleet. Long tour of a frontier shared with the **Veyli**. Nobody aboard is a soldier; there
  are no weapons aboard and nobody talks about fighting.
- **The Concord:** the human union that runs the Survey Service. "The Concord academy" trains officers (the Captain's
  daughter is there). Concord couriers, inspectors and charts-office staff visit as guests.
- **The Veyli:** proud, ritual-minded, courteous, hard to read. **Envoy Saruth** lives aboard (Deck 2, guest cabin E).
  Knot cord on the door = visitors by invitation only; a new knot each month aboard. Takes tea kneeling at a low table
  at the same hour every day. Veyli ships are rare visitors. Holt's distrust of the Veyli is voiced and argued with,
  never a slur, never a joke about them as a people.
- **The shuttle:** the **Tern**, on its pad in the Deck 5 shuttle bay. You arrive on it.
- **Other ships, by name:** the **Halcyon** (another survey ship; where transfers often go). Ask before adding more.
- **Places people come from** (only these, for now): **Port Halloran** (a port city; rain; Brennan's old restaurant),
  **Hesper Dock** (a big station-port: market stalls, boats, rain), **Kesh**, **Lisk** (a place: "letters home to Lisk"), **Valo**, **Coriel**,
  **Marra Station** (rebuilt twice; Kim collects its old maps), **New Aberdeen**, **the Ostrander colonies**, **the Tevan belt**, and Tomas's **farm moon** (unnamed: "the farm moon", a sky that is always the same
  grey). Pell's home town and the others not listed are unnamed until a writer needs one: ask.
- **Time:** a ship day is 18 waking hours of play; 06:00 breakfast, 08:00 shift, dinner from 18:00. The day shift runs
  08:00-16:30; the night shift is its own world (Bunk 8, the inner cabins). Tea break at 10:30 in Works.
- **The ship's year:** 364 days, 52 weeks, Day 1 a Monday. Four seasons named for the garden: **Sowing** (days 1-91),
  **Greening** (92-182), **Harvest** (183-273), **Lanterns** (274-364). You arrive on **Day 0**, a Sunday, the last evening
  of Lanterns. Founding Day is day 100; the quiet watch (a quiet while at the windows for the people you miss) is day 300.
- **Days off:** everyone has two in a row; many at weekends, not all (the night shift has its own pattern). See the crew
  table. "Rest day" and "day off" are both fine.
- **Money:** credits. The crew store (Deck 3) sells small things. No other currency, no banks, no brands.
- **Talking home:** the **call-home booths** on Deck 3 (relay lag; calls drop; there's a queue after storms) and
  **Mail** on your device. Some crew write letters on paper. Messages "from home" arrive and wait unread.
- **What the game doesn't have** (never mention): photos, cameras, phones (your **device** is the only personal
  gadget: Mail, Calendar, Roster, Jobs, Needs, Memos, Wallet), weapons, money other than credits, pets aboard (pets
  live back home), alcohol by brand (the lounge has a bar; drinks are just "a drink"), real-world anything.
- **Mild words only:** "blast" is the ship's only swear. "Oh, for stars' sake" is fine. Nothing stronger.
