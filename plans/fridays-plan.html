Short Fridays and the Captain's Address

A plan for your decision of 2026-10-20: "Friday is good. Let's make it so everyone gets off work early on Friday. Get off at 1530, then meeting at 1600. Then dinner at 1700. We need dialog explaining the short work day Fridays." Every Friday the day shift stops at 15:30, the Captain speaks to the whole crew at 16:00, and dinner is at 17:00, as it already is. The first is Friday Day 5, in your arrival week. Planned by PP1 for lead 21 on claude/integration (0.64.2 to 0.64.4). Nothing is built yet. Revision 2 (later that day): your answers are in. The address is held in the rec deck auditorium, it's expected, there are four full addresses following the progression, and both happen when an event is on. One new question is at the bottom, about Day 12, which falls during the ion storm.

At a glance

  • About 9 turns across seven parts (A to G below), all behind one switch, SHORT_FRIDAY. With it off the game is unchanged. Turning it on is its own reviewed step.
  • Dinner is already 17:00-20:30 (MEALS.dinner, section 14 line 9179), so the meal doesn't move. What changes is that about 60-70 people walk in at once instead of in ones and twos.
  • The Friday afternoon is free today. No tradition runs between 15:30 and 17:00. Friday's own traditions (Works Friday tea 18:45, the Music jam 19:45, Tap night 20:00, New hands' tea 18:40 on Day 5) stay where they are.
  • Who comes. 50 day-shift crew work on a Friday (9 have it as a day off), plus 11 of the night shift. So the address draws about 60-70 people, plus you.
  • Where decided: the auditorium: the rec deck auditorium (deck R, between 4 and 5), with 76 seats and a lectern. Everyone expected (about 60-70) can sit. Afterwards the crew ride up to Deck 3 for dinner at 17:00, in waves (part D).
  • The ion storm keeps no all-hands in the auditorium (settled). The season's all-hands in the auditorium (the first Monday of each season, 17:45) is a separate, existing tradition and stays.

C. The address itself (section 18, content/)

Traditions engineer (the tradition), writers (the words, four full addresses), dialogue engineer (rotating parts) · 2½ turns · events_check, join_check, lines_check, content_gen --check

A weekly tradition, captains-address.trad, built like the existing season all-hands (rec-all-hands). It brings people together through the gathering spots, not the broadcast. captainAddress() (section 18, line 35495) only sends a banner and Mail today.

  • when: weekly friday, at: 16:00-16:40, where: 7 room Auditorium: rec:seat.
  • Captain Marrow at the lectern (rec:lectern), Commander Vance beside her, as in the season's all-hands.
  • who: everyone 90%, the night shift 50%, the skeleton never. The skeleton gets the banner and the Mail.

How it plays decided: bubbles:

  • The Captain's lines are speech bubbles over her. Your comment asked for substantial addresses, so the length is set by reading time. About 12-16 lines at one every 5 real seconds is 60-80 real seconds. At the ship's 0.6 ship minutes a real second, that's 36-48 ship minutes, so the address runs 16:00-16:40 (it was 16:25).
  • The crowd's bubbles are hushed while she speaks.
  • Tap to join, as with any tradition.
  • Anyone not there gets the banner and the Mail ("All hands: the Captain's Friday address").
  • It ends with "Dinner's at five. Enjoy your weekend, those of you who have one."

What she says decided: the shape: the same shape every week, with parts that change. Fridays 5, 12, 19 and 26 also get an address written in full that follows the progression (the outline below).

  1. Hello, and the week's headline: one line, from a pool.
  2. The week behind: something that really happened, read when she speaks (never by the crew's planner). The ship's health from the machines; a repair that mattered; a tradition's winner (the tasting table, the Cup).
  3. Welcomes and goodbyes from the rota's arrivals and leavers (ROSTER_ROTATIONS). In week one: you.
  4. A department nod, taking turns week by week: Works, the galley, the watch, survey, the infirmary, the garden.
  5. The week ahead: next week's traditions, and a ship-wide event if one is coming.
  6. Now and then, you: your name on the beam, your first solo repair, a promotion.
  7. The close.

When a ship-wide event falls on a Friday, both happen (decided; see "When an event is on").

When an event is on (decided: both)

  • Day 12, the ion storm (Days 11-15). The address goes ahead at 16:00 and is the Captain's storm update; the storm's own phases keep their times. If a storm phase falls between 16:00 and 16:40, it waits until the address ends, or the address waits for an emergency phase. The storm's design was settled without an all-hands in the auditorium; this is the regular Friday address, not a storm all-hands. Question fp-v2-q1 checks that's what you want.
  • Day 26, the visit's warning. The address happens and the visit's warning keeps its own time and form. The Captain refers to it ("Commander Vance will brief you on the details"), and Four weeks aboard is at 18:40 after dinner.
  • Days 7, 16 and 18 (Crossing the Line on a Sunday, the comet on a Tuesday, the summons on a Thursday) aren't Fridays, so there's no overlap. The events plan's calendar (F0) marks Friday 15:30-17:00 as taken, so a future event is never put on top of the address.
  • Pillar 5's night shifts (Days 19-20): you're excused from the address on Day 19, and the address wishes the night watch well.

Order and size. A (1½) and B (1½-2) first, together, since they share the 15:30 end; then C (2) and D (½-1); then E (1), F (¼) and G (½). About 9 turns, all behind SHORT_FRIDAY.
