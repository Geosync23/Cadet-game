# Pillar 3: the ship gets a voice

*A proposal for you to approve. The full plan is in `plans/pillar3-plan.md`. Nothing here is built yet.*

## The idea in a paragraph

The Wren already works: the galley cooks, machines wear out, the Works crew fix them and so can you. Pillar 3 gives that ship a voice. Your **personal device** becomes the one screen you use for everything. The **crew become people you know**, who remember you, chat to you, invite you places and ask you for favours. The first **ship-wide events** begin, like the ion storm: a few days when the whole ship changes and everyone talks about it. There are no cutscenes and no mission markers, and the game never stops for a story. You live through each event as one of the crew and decide how much to help.

## What you'd see and do

- Walking through the mess, you overhear someone say "Sato hasn't left the chart room since last night", and later find it in your news feed.
- You tap Pell and get a short chat, with buttons to chat, ask about something, offer help, or meet up.
- You make an appointment to go to the gym with Tomas at 18:00, and he's there.
- A friend asks you to fix the lamp in their cabin, and it shows up in your jobs.
- The Captain's face appears across the top of the screen with a ship-wide announcement. The crew around you stop and look up for a moment, then carry on.

## Your device

It opens from a corner button or a swipe up. On a phone it slides up from the bottom and leaves the ship visible; on the unfolded TriFold it sits at the side. **Time keeps running while it's open.**

| App | What it's for |
| --- | --- |
| Schedule | Your day: shift, meals, appointments, gatherings |
| Appointments | Meet a crew member, see the infirmary, talk to the Chief. Crew answer from their real schedule and turn up |
| Maps | Every deck, where you are, find a room or a friend, tap to walk there |
| Memos | Your own notes |
| Mail | Messages from the ship, the crew, the Captain and home |
| Calendar | Ship traditions, birthdays, and events once they're announced |
| Needs | Your five needs, with their bonuses and penalties |
| Jobs | Your work, plus favours friends ask of you |
| *Proposed:* Crew | Everyone you've met, how well you know them, what they've told you |
| *Proposed:* Ship | The ship's health and the latest news |
| *Proposed:* Camera | Take photos and keep them in a photo log |
| *Proposed:* Record | Your Works progress and what the Chief thinks of you |

**Your design for the device can come whenever it's ready.** The apps are built so that the look is a separate piece we can swap. Until then, everything works in a plain style.

## How ambient events work: the ion storm, through your eyes

**Day 1, morning.** Nothing official yet. At breakfast a Survey officer says the sensors are "doing something odd". By dinner half the mess is talking about it. A Works notice lands in your Mail: *storm prep, secure loose fittings, top up filters.* New jobs appear on the board, such as securing the bow lounge's glassware and carrying battery packs to the bridge. You take two of them, or you don't. That's up to you.

**Day 2, 14:00.** You're fixing a lamp on Deck 4 when the screen jolts, every light dips, and then they start to flicker. The Captain's announcement appears across the top: *"We're in it. Expect flickers and no calls home for a while. Look after each other."* The call-home booths close, the lounge is shut, and the mess is quiet. Bridge crew are on double shifts.

**Day 3.** It gets harder. Relays fail, and one deck at a time drops to dim emergency lighting. The chef makes storm stew and sends trays up to the bridge; you can push the cart. People are tired and snappy when you tap them. Each job you do keeps the lights on for someone.

**Day 4, mid-morning.** The all-clear is announced. Calls home come back on, and there's a queue at the booths all afternoon. In the evening there's a party in the bow lounge.

**Afterwards.** Nobody shows you a score. If it went **smoothly**, a photo of the storm crew goes up in the Works tea corner and people thank you. If it went **badly**, there's a scorch mark in the relay room, the ship is grumpy for a couple of days, and then it recovers. **You can't fail.** If you did your part you may get a commendation, which counts toward promotion. If you slept through it, the crew coped, and you hear all about it the next day.

**Big moments are never missed or forced.** Some are felt on every deck at once (the jolt), some are the Captain's announcements, and some are gatherings announced in advance (the party, a delegation stepping off a shuttle). If you're there, you see it. If not, people tell you about it.

**After the storm:** a **diplomatic visit**, with Veyli guests in the visitors' cabins and formal dinners, and then **something in the garden**: a creature eats the crops, half the ship hunts it, and it ends up as the ship's pet. Later ideas include illness, a nebula, salvage, and a festival with the whole crew on the plaza.

## Getting to know the crew

- Every crew member is a **stranger, acquaintance, colleague, friend or close friend** to you. You get closer by working together, eating together, chatting (once a day each) and doing favours. Friendships don't fade.
- Friends **say hello, come and find you, invite you places**, and put in a good word with the Chief.
- The crew have **friendships and small rivalries with each other**, which change who sits together and what they gossip about.
- **Tomas, Pell and Rina** get the first personal storylines.

## Moving up

- Summoned to the **Captain's ready room** after you do well in an event.
- Taken by the Chief to a **briefing at the conference table**.
- Invited to **tea in an officer's cabin**.
- An invitation to the **Captain's table** for dinner.
- **Progress talks** in the Chief's office.

## The order of work

1. The device, relationships and chats, and appointments, all built side by side. We also run the real-phone test with you.
2. **Check-in 1 with you:** try the new device and the social life.
3. The ion storm from start to finish, and the first visit to the ready room.
4. **Check-in 2 with you:** play through a whole storm.
5. Then the visit, the garden creature, and the festival.

## Thirteen questions for you

1. Should the device's look be a separate, swappable piece, so it works now and your design drops in later?
2. Add the extra apps: Crew, Ship, Camera, Record (and a Wallet, and Music once the game has sound)?
3. Should Maps show where someone is only once you know them, or once they're on duty somewhere public?
4. If you miss an appointment, should it cost nothing beyond a remark next time?
5. Should events reach you only through chatter, Mail, the boards and the Captain, with no markers or cutscenes and the camera never taken away?
6. Should results come as smoothly, bumpily or roughly, told as story and small traces on the ship, with no scores and no failing?
7. Are the three kinds of big moment right: felt everywhere, the Captain's announcement, and announced gatherings?
8. Event order: ion storm, then the diplomatic visit, then the garden creature, with the festival held back until the phone test says big crowds are OK?
9. Should ordinary visitors stay in the guest cabins about once a week, as well as the event visitors?
10. Are the friendship tiers, the short chats and crew friendships with each other right, with Tomas, Pell and Rina first?
11. Should the ready room, briefings, officers' cabins and the Captain's table open by invitation, for the time of the visit only?
12. Add more gym equipment now, and give the game some simple sounds soon?
13. OK to bring in two new helpers (one for the device, one for social life), with the traditions helper growing into ship-wide events?
