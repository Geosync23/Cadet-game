2. The diplomatic visit: story, guests and threads

Four Veyli come aboard for two nights. They are proud, ritual-minded and courteous, hard to read, and very sure of who they are. The Wren is a guest in their sky, and for two days they are guests in the Wren's corridors. Both sides know it, and the visit is about being good at both at once. The words keep to the old rules: the Wren travels the reach "as guests", never by anyone's leave or permission; nobody aboard is a soldier; Holt's distrust is his own, about one day, and never a word against the Veyli as a people.

The story: "Two at the door"

Like the storm's "Sixty by lamplight" and the Line's "The one on watch", the visit has one small story with one choice for you. Ithra, the delegation's pilot, watches every door. So does Chief Holt. On Wednesday evening they end up standing at the same door on Deck 2, either side of nobody, for forty minutes, without a word. Kofi Mensah asks you to walk past. You can say a door has two sides, fetch Envoy Saruth, or leave them to it. What you chose shows at Thursday's dinner (who stands where), at the farewell (what Ithra says to Holt) and in the crew's talk afterwards. Every ending is kind: even left alone, the two of them end the visit with a nod.

The guests

Speaker Teshavi

Leads the delegation; speaks for the Veyli council. Visitors' suite, Deck 2.

Grave, slow, ceremonial; every word chosen and long silences meant. Kind to juniors in a grand way. Brought the Captain a gift in seven knots and will not say what's in it. Sits in the garden at 07:00 in silence; you may sit too.

Kesrin

The Speaker's aide. Guest cabin 4-01.

Quick, polite, a little breathless; carries the schedule in their head and a tea set in a satchel; writes everything down, jokes included. Secretly delighted by the vending machine on Deck 3. Goes to quiz night.

Moleth

Scholar of human ships. Guest cabin 4-02.

Eager and formal, all questions: "May I ask..." and then four more. Forty questions for Chief Calder. Wants the engine room more than anything. Ambushes the Open door tea and Calder's chief's talk, and eats in the mess with the crew.

Ithra

Pilot of the delegation's shuttle; watches the doors. Guest cabin 4-03.

Almost no words: one or two, then a nod. Runs laps of the promenade at 06:00 (you can run with them). The other half of "Two at the door".

Envoy Saruth

Lives aboard (cabin E, Deck 2). Not one of the four.

Formal and exact, dry a sentence late. Old friend of the Speaker (students of the cord together). Goes between the two peoples without taking a side, and at the comet a week later ties the comet knot.

The Wren's side

Who carries it.

Captain Marrow hosts. Commander Vance runs it, called in on both his days off (Wed and Thu) and says so. Chief Holt and Kofi Mensah keep the corridors. Brennan cooks, Lukas helps. Lucia Ferreira reads the Veyli hail and sings at the farewell with Femi Rossi. Zeke Torres gives the engine tour. Tove Cruz has the herbs. Sato (Survey) made the chart that is the Wren's gift.

7. The comet, hour by hour

One gentle evening a week after the guests have gone, on the day of Calder's question: the end of the career's first stretch. It has no threads, no score and no commendation (like the Line), and it always goes well. It ties the month together: the comet's blue tail is blown by the same wind that gave the Wren the storm, Saruth ties a Veyli knot for it, and if the visit went smoothly the crew's knots go onto the Speaker's cord sculpture.
