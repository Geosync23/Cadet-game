Every week a little different

You asked for each week to have different traditions: some can repeat, like gym club, but more fresh ones coming in. And for the ship-wide events, the diplomatic visit now comes before the comet viewing. This page shows what the first twelve weeks look like today, what would change, and what the event swap touches. Nothing is built. Answer at the bottom.

3. Traditions: nine fresh ones

One new tradition starts in each of weeks 2 to 10, so something fresh arrives every week (weeks 5 to 12 also keep their 0.92.6 ones). They come back on 3- and 6-week rhythms rather than every 4 weeks, which is what stops the weeks repeating in a 4-week loop. Each is in a room the Wren already has, with a host from the roster, and each was checked against what's already in that room and hour on its weeks. The builder confirms each host is free in their own day plan before it goes in.

5. The ship-wide events: the visit first, then the comet

Today the plan is Crossing the Line (Day 7), the ion storm (Days 11-15), the comet viewing (Tuesday Day 16, short) and the diplomatic visit (from Friday Day 26, the finale). Only the first two are switched on. The comet exists as a draft, and the visit is built but switched off. So the swap changes no play today, except the words of two Friday addresses that already mention the comet and the visit.

The one real decision: how long the visit is

A (recommended): a short visit, Tuesday to Friday, Days 16-19. Tuesday 16: the warning Mail and prep jobs. Wednesday 17: the Veyli come aboard at 10:00 in the shuttle bay. Thursday 18: the formal dinner. Friday 19: the farewell at 09:00, before the Friday hold, so the Captain's address that afternoon can talk about it. It clears out before your night shifts, the Captain's table (Day 20), Theo's send-off and Basil's arrival (Day 21). Building the shorter shape is the main work: the built visit is nine days long.
B: the built nine-day visit, Days 16-24. No reshaping, but it runs straight across the Senior Officer scenes (Days 16-17), your night shifts, the Captain's table (same room as the visit's dinners), Theo's send-off in the same shuttle bay, the bridge invite (Commander Vance hosts both), and the Pell, Basil and Zeke stories. Nothing in the game stops those clashes today. Risky.
Comet viewing after it, around Day 26: either Friday 26 at 21:30, after Calder's question and the four-weeks-aboard tea (tap night would end early or pause), or Saturday 27 at 21:30 in place of that week's observatory night, which fits it naturally. It becomes the end of the career's first stretch, instead of the visit.
