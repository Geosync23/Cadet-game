Step 5: Senior Officer, the build spec

This page turns the agreed plan (version 3.1, your answers of 26 October) into pieces the builders can start on. For each piece: what it is, exactly which parts of the game file it touches, its switch, which builder seat it suits, the checks it must pass, and the words the head writer must write. Nothing here changes the design you agreed; where the game needed a small decision the plan didn't make, it's listed under "Judgement calls" so you can overrule it.

PiecesSix building pieces and the words: the career core first (small), then the promotion, power and relays, shutdowns, the sensor array and the head-scratchers side by side.

SizeAbout 26 turns in all, shared by four builders; the longest seat (the Works career engineer: the core, then the promotion) has about 7.

1. The ground rules for every piece

  • Off means today's game. With P5_STEP5 off, cadet_test is ALL IDENTICAL against the build before it, and every day_check day is the same. Every piece also has its own switch, so a reviewer can turn one piece off and see the rest.
  • Never touch the first promotion. Section 24p (the Tier 2 test and the Officer promotion) keeps working exactly as now. Step 5 reuses its helpers (promoNotice, promoSay, promoMail, promoCerTimes, promoGuests, promoBusy) by calling them, and only adds one-line, typeof-guarded hooks into 24p where the plan needs them (listed per piece). SHIP.works.test stays the Tier 2 record.
  • The planner rule. The crew's day plans read only what's fixed for that day. Shutdown bookings (piece S5-4) are settled at 22:00 the evening before, copying the rec deck's recPlanShut / recSettled pattern in section 17 (a plan made before the record settles is marked and made again once it does).
  • Nothing new at load calls R(). Choices come from schedU keys; section 24's note reserves 852-854 as spare: S5-1 hands 852 to S5-2 (which friends come), 853 to S5-4 (who is warned in the test), 854 to S5-6 (the old-mysteries board's order). Any more come from a new key range agreed with the sim engineer.
  • Phones first. Every colour through col(), no coplanar faces, the 450 draw-call budget at phone and TriFold sizes. New props are merged meshes.
  • Every new button works with a game controller (section 9 of this page): real buttons inside panels the controller already walks, nothing held or fast, nothing that only vibrates.
  • Each turn, as CLAUDE.md says: small exact-string edits, new code in your slot, a row in plans/build-table.md, your piece's checks then bash check_fast.sh, commit and push; never the two build-number lines.

11. Judgement calls (overrule any of them)

  • The Tier 3 offer's second need. The plan says "Relied on and Power safety"; the game has no Power safety row. This spec adds a short one (question 2); the other choice is to use Team jobs (Day 11), which every Officer has done by Day 14.
  • The test's two warnings. The two people come from the junction branch's rooms on that day's fixed roster; if one can't be found within 30 minutes, Calder tells them, so the test never stalls.
  • Shutdown branches, not whole junctions. A shutdown turns off one branch (one to three rooms), never a deck, so a mistake costs a grumble, not half the ship. The fed rooms don't change their lights (only the junction room goes dark): per-room emergency lighting doesn't exist today, and adding it risks draw calls and pixel changes on every deck.
  • Step 6's held hours are moved now. Leaving the old night rows on Days 19-20 would keep the Captain's table early on Day 20 and block the guard there; the plan wants them gone in Step 5.
  • Four new sub-sections, 24u-24x. Each mechanic owns its own section like 24a-24t; Step 6 gets a section 28 because the letters run out.
  • The words come last but nobody waits. Builders use stand-ins; the head writer starts with the promotion's words, the ones you'll see first.

12. Your questions

1. Play the Senior Officer promotion early?

S5-2 is about a day and a half in. It can get its own full round and a Dev link (with #p5s5) so you can play the offer, the test, the result, the bar and the toast before the rest of Step 5 is done. The live game doesn't change either way.

Recommended: yes, a Dev link after S5-2. Your notes then shape the Engine Specialist promotion too. It costs one extra round (about 30 minutes of the runners).

2. "Power safety" before the Tier 3 test

The plan's offer needs "Relied on" as an Officer and Power safety, but there's no Power safety lesson in the game.

Recommended: a short lesson with Calder at a Deck 6 junction, offered from Day 12 (about 20 minutes, watch-only like P3). It sets up the junction you'll shut down in the test. The other choice: count Team jobs instead, and say nothing about power until the test.

3. Booking a shutdown over a meal or a tradition

The planning screen shows who'd be affected at each hour. What if you pick dinner, or the gym class?

Recommended: allowed, with consequences. The screen marks meal and tradition hours plainly; if you book one anyway, the crew grumble the next day and your reputation dips a little, and it can turn up later as a P9 clash. The other choice: those hours can't be picked at all.
