## The lead's ideas the user liked ("I like everything else")
- Arrival in the evening; escort walks ahead talking, a follow arrow, you stay in control (never a full cutscene): shuttle ramp, haulway, glimpse of the Works bay, lift, your bunk (drop bag; the escort hands you your device, which introduces it), dinner in the mess with the escort; Chief Calder stops by the table ("be at the board at 06:00"), which also gives her a personality; bed ends the first night.
- First week, taught by people, not pop-ups: Day 1 shadow shift (the escort trains you on one real job end to end: board, part, machine shop, repair, turn-in); Day 2 Calder gives your first solo job; the cook explains meals, someone invites you to chat, Mail arrives, etc. An optional "Settling in" checklist on the device. First weekend: an invitation to a tradition (teaches days off). A skip-intro option on later new games.
- Dialogue: a voice card for each of the 75 crew (how they talk, what they care about, running jokes); overheard two-person conversations as you pass; reactions to real sim events (storm, harvest, a repair you just did); story arcs for 6-10 main characters over weeks.
- Deeper social: crew remember what you did; crew-to-crew friendships change; maybe small gifts and crew visiting your bunk.
- More traditions: weekly, monthly, seasonal; tie to existing systems (garden harvest festival, etc.).
- Writers mostly produce text (data), not code, so they can work in parallel; a head writer collects their work.
