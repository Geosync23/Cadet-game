- Dialogue: a voice card for each of the 75 crew (how they talk, what they care about, running jokes); overheard two-person conversations as you pass; reactions to real sim events (storm, harvest, a repair you just did); story arcs for 6-10 main characters over weeks.

- More traditions: weekly, monthly, seasonal; tie to existing systems (garden harvest festival, etc.).
- Writers mostly produce text (data), not code, so they can work in parallel; a head writer collects their work.
