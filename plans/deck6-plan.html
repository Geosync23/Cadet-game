Deck 6: Engineering

Sketch 3, as built in 0.11.0. Bow points forward, port side is on the left when facing it. The bottom of the ship: short, narrow and warm, with a hum you feel through your boots. The engine room fills the flat stern and the reactor fills the bow. Between them is the foot of the spine, an overlook and the engine watch room looking onto the drive coils, and engine control looking into the reactor. You can walk the engine room from day one; engine control, the reactor, the junctions and the crawlways stay shut until tiers 3 and 4. Tap any room. Rooms ringed in gold are new ideas, not in the design doc yet.
Deck 6 at a glanceThe engine room aft, with three drive coils and the drive housing across the stern, watched from the overlook and the watch room, and open to walk through from the overlook. The reactor core is in the bow, watched from engine control. Two power junctions sit by the spine, and the ship's lowest crawlways run along both hulls from the engine room to the reactor. The engine watch room sits at the south end of the overlook, with its own window onto the coils. Open questions I had to guess at
  • When it opens. The doc says Deck 6 opens at tiers 3 and 4. I've assumed the lift comes here from the start, with the lobby, overlook, watch room and engine room open (the user asked for the engine room to be walkable in 0.11.0). Repairs there are still tier 4 team jobs; locking by tier comes later with the story work.
  • An overlook on one level. The build has one floor height per deck, so the overlook is a gallery behind glass, level with the engine room floor, with a door straight onto it. A real drop to a lower coil floor would need new kit.
  • Who works here. The engine watch room is a new idea. In 0.11.0 it moved from a 2 × 3 corner by engine control to a 4 × 4 room at the overlook's south end, looking onto the coils. Is there an engine watch on both shifts, and are they Works crew?
  • Crawlways. They're sealed void strips for now, like Deck 5's pipe galleries. They'll want the low ceiling and crouch-walk when crawlways arrive.
  • Reactor door. The reactor's shielded door opens off a short alcove at the end of the lobby, not out of engine control. That way no room sits behind another locked room, and a tier 4 team can reach it without engine control being unlocked.
  • Tiers. Both power junctions and engine control are tier 3; the reactor is tier 4, and so are big repairs in the engine room. Should one junction open at tier 2?
  • Shape. 36 × 12 with a blunt bow, as if the lower hull sweeps up toward the bow under Deck 5. It runs from world x −21 to 12.6, inside Deck 5's −21 to 21.6. The stern lines up with Deck 5's bay doors.
