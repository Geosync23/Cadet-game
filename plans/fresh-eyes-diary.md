# Fresh-eyes diary: Days 0-2 as a new player

FE1, the fresh-eyes player. Build: claude/integration, 0.64.15 (2b4a05d). Phone size 390 x 844, touch, Chromium. I play only from what is on screen; no code reading, no dev shortcuts.

**How bad:** *blocks me* (I could not go on, or lost something I was meant to do), *confusing* (I worked it out, or guessed, but a new player might not), *small* (looks or wording).

**Speed note.** In this test machine the game draws about 3 frames a second, so ship time runs about 6 times slower than on a phone (about 10 real seconds a ship minute). Walking and talking are played in real time. When I was only waiting, I skipped ahead and say so ("Skip:").

Screenshots: `plans/fresh-eyes/`.

---

## Day 0, Sunday (arrival)

**16:30, Shuttle bay.** The manifest card asks for my name; I type "Robin" and tap "Sign and go aboard". Clear and pleasant.

1. **16:30, the first message is wrong for where I am.** The bottom card says "Deck 4, CSV Wren. You wake in Bunk 1, in the junior wing..." while the top-left says Shuttle bay, Deck 5, and I'm on a shuttle. It goes away when the pilot speaks, but for a moment the game tells me I'm in bed on Deck 4. *Confusing.* (Seen in the text of the first screen, before signing.)

2. **16:30-16:41, the pilot's line never leaves.** "Dev Raman, the pilot: Down gently. Ramp's opening. Welcome to the Wren." stays in the bottom card for about 40 ship minutes, all the way through the shuttle bay, the haulway and the lift lobby, under everything Zeke says. I kept thinking it was something I still had to answer. *Small.* (`d0-09-bubble-hides-player.png`)

3. **16:31, the first tip covers the HUD.** "TIP: Tap where you want to go, or press and hold to walk." sits over the Needs bar and Tracked button. Fine, but I could not see Tracked until it went. *Small.* (`d0-01-tip-covers-hud.png`)

4. **16:32-16:40, I didn't know whether to follow Zeke or wait.** Zeke greets me and talks ("Hello, cadet. I'm Zeke Torres..."), standing still. No arrow, no "follow Zeke". I stood and listened for 8 ship minutes until he started to walk. Then I walked after him. It worked, but I was never told "follow him". *Confusing.*

5. **16:40, Tracked list.** Opening Tracked on Day 0 shows "Now: Reading corner, Bow lounge, Deck 3", then Dinner 17:00, Sunday long table 18:00... I didn't know why "Reading corner" is "Now", or if I was meant to go there instead of following Zeke. "On and coming up" was clear for the rest. *Confusing.* (`d0-02-tracked-list.png`)

6. **16:51, the arrow's label has a small letter.** "Take the lift to Deck 4: zeke Torres" (small z). *Small.* (`d0-03-lift-label-lowercase.png`)

7. **16:51-17:23, I could not work out how to ride the lift. (Biggest problem of Day 0.)** Zeke says "Four, please. Go on, the panel's all yours." The arrow points into the lift. I walked into the lift car and tapped the lift panel on the wall several times, tapped the floor of the car, tapped the doors, pressed and held inside the car: nothing. No message, no deck list. Meanwhile Zeke stood beside me and the clock ran. After about 20 ship minutes I tapped the "+" button top right, hoping it was a menu: it said "New game. Tap again to start over." (that frightened me; see 8). I then found the **spine ladder** next to the lift by accident and it gave me a deck list; I tapped what I thought was Deck 4 and climbed **up to Deck 2** instead (see 9), then back down to Deck 4. On Deck 4 Zeke was waiting by the lift and carried on as if nothing happened. Later, on Deck 4 at 19:14, I found the lift works when you tap the small keypad on the **outside** wall, next to the lift door: the "Main lift, choose a deck" list. On Deck 5 I never found that keypad; I was tapping inside the car. *Blocks me* (on Deck 5 I only got off it by climbing the ladder). (`d0-04-lift-panel-nothing.png`, `d0-15-lift-keypad-small.png`, `d0-14-lift-panel-found.png`)
   - What I expected: tapping anywhere in or at the lift opens the deck list, or the arrow points at the panel itself, not at the lift.

8. **17:17, the "+" button is New game.** A round "+" in the top-right corner, next to the people button, reads as "add" or "more". Tapping it says "New game. Tap again to start over." One more tap and my game would be gone. *Confusing* (and risky).  (`d0-06-plus-is-new-game.png`)

9. **17:31, the spine ladder took me to the wrong deck.** I tapped the ladder; the screen said "Deck 2, Officer's Quarters - Science. Up the spine ladder, 4 decks." I had meant to go down to Deck 4. On the ladder list I can't remember choosing Deck 2; I tapped near the middle of the list. The list itself is clear ("Up / Down", "2 decks down"). *Confusing.* (`d0-07-ladder-to-deck2.png`, `d0-08-ladder-menu.png`)

10. **17:06, "Help?" on a person's card.** On Zainab's card I tapped "Help?" expecting help for me (I was lost at the lift). It's me offering to help her: "Kind of you. Nothing right now." A new player who is stuck will tap "Help?" for help. *Confusing.* (`d0-05-help-button.png`)

11. **17:56-18:30, the bunk room.** Zeke: "Eight bunk rooms. You're in One. First, we drop your bag." I followed him into Bunk 5 by mistake (he walked through it), then to Bunk 1. The arrow "Put your bag in your wardrobe" appeared in Bunk 1. I tapped my wardrobe and nothing happened for a while; then "Zeke puts your bag in your wardrobe for you." So I didn't really do it myself. *Small.*

12. **18:31-18:40, the device hand-over.** Nice. "YOUR DEVICE · Zeke Torres · 1/4 ... Go on". The reply choices ("What do I do first?") are fine. Zeke: "Dinner. Always dinner first." Then two instructions at once: the arrow "Take the lift to Deck 3: zeke Torres" and a tag "Tap your device". *Small.* (`d0-11-two-instructions.png`)

13. **18:41-19:00, the tour ran out the dinner time.** After the device, Zeke stood at the edge of the bunk room and didn't move; the arrow said "Take the lift to Deck 3: zeke Torres". I walked to the lift lobby; he stayed behind. The arrow kept pointing at a spot by a wall that I could not reach (the arrow ring on the right edge, by the bunk room wall). By 19:00 I had a Mail: **"Missed you at dinner. Torres has you tomorrow; ten to nine, the Works bay."** (from Calder). I hadn't skipped dinner; I was following the tour, and the tour never got me there. *Blocks me* (I missed the welcome dinner the tour was taking me to). (`d0-12-missed-dinner-mail-bubble-over-device.png`)
   - The Tracked list still said "Dinner: Now, the mess, Deck 3" after the Mail said I'd missed it.

14. **19:03, a speech bubble covered my device.** Zeke's bubble "How was today, honestly? Got a minute to tell me?" sat on top of the open device screen, over the Mail I was reading. *Small.* (`d0-12-...png`)

15. **19:06, two bubbles for one line.** Tomas Lind's line showed twice at once: once as a normal speech bubble and once as a dark "Tomas: ..." bubble just under it. *Small.* (`d0-13-double-bubble.png`)

16. **19:16, Deck 3 arrival card.** "Deck 3, Commons. The promenade runs stern to bow. Mess hall and galley to port, the garden aft, the lounge in the bow." Port, aft, bow, stern are fine for a ship game, but I couldn't tell which way port was on screen. *Small.* (`d0-20-deck3-arrival.png`)

I tapped a chair at a round table: "Seat. You sit down. Tap anywhere to get up." No eating; Food stayed at 47-48. When I tapped elsewhere to get up, the tray was gone. I did it a second time (another tray, another chair, then a bench at the long table): the same. Food went from 48 to 46 over half an hour. I never ate on Day 0. *Blocks me* (food). (`d0-16-sat-with-tray-no-eating.png`, `d0-18-tray-vanished.png`)
   - Also: the "You sit down" message stayed on screen long after I had stood up and walked away.
   - Tapping the servery also opened the galley hand's (Tariq Varga's) card each time, because he stands behind it. *Small.* (`d0-19-servery-opens-card.png`)

18. **19:52, bubbles cover the clock.** Bo Mbeki's and Mateo Navarro's bubbles sat over the top-left clock and the Device and Needs buttons. *Small.* (`d0-17-bubble-covers-clock.png`)

(Note for the lead, not a game problem: my test browser was restarted at 20:40 and the game reloaded from its own hourly save at 20:26, Main lift Deck 4. "Welcome back" said so. Tracked was cleared by the reload, which is fine.)
