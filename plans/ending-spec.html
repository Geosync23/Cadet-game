1. The offer: who, where, how long, changing your mind

  • Who and where: the Captain, in her ready room, with Calder: Thursday Day 32 at 16:00, a Captain's scene (the guard's captain kind). The letter is from Concord headquarters.
  • The Admiral: in the game he's the Captain's fish, and her notes say what he is is never explained. So the offer comes from headquarters through the Captain, never from an admiral in person; the fish gets his usual look.
  • What's offered: a post at headquarters (question 3), its wording coloured by your answer to Calder's question (Step 6). If Step 6 isn't on, or you never answered, there's a plain version.
  • How long: until Saturday 20:00. You tell the Captain in person (her "About the transfer" button, wherever she is). If you haven't by 19:30 Saturday, she comes to find you and asks.
  • Changing your mind: you can tell her again any time until her address at the line on Sunday (about 15:00), as often as you like. After the address it's settled and the crew know. "Not sure yet" on Saturday keeps it open until the address, where she asks you at the front, before the crowd, with Stay and Go only.
  • A slower player: the crossing back is Sunday Day 35 for everyone (the ship's course doesn't wait). The offer comes after your career's end (Calder's question). If that's later than Day 32, the offer comes the Thursday after, the answer is due the Saturday after, and a Go leaves on the Monday after (the Tern meets a Concord courier). The scenes are the same; the oath is still yours on Day 35.

2. After you choose: the endgame, and the end

  • Stay: from Monday Day 36 the game goes on for good.
    • No more ship-wide events: the calendar has nothing after the crossing back, and no new rows are ever added once you've stayed.
    • No more promotions: the ladder is finished.
    • The traditions, jobs, head-scratchers, friendships, rotations of the crew and every daily system go on.
    • A flag, end.stay, unlocks the endgame pool's lines (section 22 gets a new line condition, "if": ["endgame"]).
  • Go: after the ramp closes, the end page, a full-screen panel over the shuttle bay, shows:
    • your name and "Senior Officer, Engine Specialist" (or your rank);
    • days aboard;
    • your ranks with their days;
    • commendations;
    • your three closest friends;
    • what you kept (the certificates, the sketches, the Line certificate's two dates);
    • your answer to Calder;
    • Zeke's last line.
    Then two buttons (your answer 1: no way back):
    • Keep this ending: the save is marked ended; loading it shows this page again.
    • Start a new game: the Saves app's slot choice.
  • No way back (your answer 1). There is no kept copy: going is final for that save. "Keep this ending" marks the save ended (SHIP.end.over); loading it shows the end page again, with the same two buttons. Other slots are untouched, so a player who wants to try the other ending uses another slot, or saves to one before Saturday (the Saves app already allows it).

1. After the goodbye, can you go back?

If you choose to go, the game ends with an end page.

Recommended: yes, one way back. The game keeps a copy from just before you first said "Go", and the end page has "Back to before my choice" (Saturday, the choice open again), "Keep this ending" and "Start a new game". The other choice: no way back from the end page (you'd use another save slot).

2. How long can you change your mind?

Recommended: until the Captain's address at the line on Sunday afternoon, after which the whole crew knows. The other choice: until the Tern's ramp on Monday (Zeke asks "Sure?" at the foot, and you can turn back).

3. What does headquarters offer?

Recommended: one post, worded by your answer to Calder's question. If you said engines, the fleet's engine office. If teaching, the cadet academy. If the bridge, the command school. If "I don't know yet", "come and find out". The other choice: the same post for everyone, a place in headquarters' engineering office.
