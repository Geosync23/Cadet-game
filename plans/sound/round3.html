Wren Sounds: Round 3

Three things to choose: which traditions get their own music, the Veyli farewell song, and sounds for what's been added since the sound build (0.93.0): the career's last steps, the Veyli vessel, the ending and a few smaller features. Everything is made live on this page, like Rounds 1 and 2. Nothing here is in the game yet.

My recommendations in short
  • Music for 9 traditions and events: Year's end, Lantern walk, Harvest table (both garden suppers), Name on the beam, Farewell supper, Lap of the Wren, Crossing the Line, Veyli welcome and The goodbye. The other four samples are maybes.
  • No music for the rest: the crew's own music nights, talks and quiet games, meals, the garden and sport (the list is below, with a switch for each in case you want one).
  • Farewell song A (bronze and drone).
  • New sounds for the knock on your door, the reactor door, the badge rack, the coils, the team calls, the Tern landing, the lathe, the bronze bar, the test fit, the offer from headquarters, Stay or Go and the clock's Undo. None for rank cards, Works tabs, the Saves app or the shop menu.

Tap a play button to hear it; tap it again to stop. Turn on a ship hum in the bottom bar to hear how things sit in the game. Pick under each one, and add a note if you like. Checking where answers save... On an iPhone, if you hear nothing, check the silent switch.

Part 1 · held since 25 Oct

Tradition music

Only some traditions get music, and you pick which. Each sample is 30 to 60 seconds in the same space-music style as the v7 tracks: pads, stars, chimes and a singing lead, no drums and no noise. In the game a tradition's music would play in its room while it's on, in place of the ship-schedule track, then hand back.

Traditions I'd leave without music

Every other tradition in the game (0.95.0), grouped by why. If you want music for one, tap its name to turn it on (it goes teal); I'll write a sample for it next round. The comet already has its own track.

Part 2 · event track

The Veyli farewell song

A one-off event track like the comet's, for the visit's farewell (Day 19, 09:00 in the shuttle bay); it could also play at the Tern's ramp when Taavek goes home. It sits low and sparse under the crew's singing for about 40 seconds. Play each version alone, then with stand-in singers (synth voices, only here to show the space left for the real ones).
