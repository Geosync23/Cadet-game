Wren Music Player

The approved music, running as it would in the game: a pretend ship clock picks music for the time of day, or work music while you're on shift. Speed the clock up to hear a whole day's schedule. This is the same player that will go into the game later.

Tap Start to begin. Sound pauses if you switch away. On an iPhone, if you hear nothing, check the silent switch. Every track can also be heard on its own on the Sound Lab.

What happened

    On your device · mockup

    The Music app

    A new app on your crew device, in the same old-school screen look as the others. Music is on by default and follows the ship's schedule. You can turn it off, set its volume, or pick a track yourself. Your choices are kept apart from the saved game, so they carry over to a new game. It works here: try it while the clock runs.

    The schedule

    Which music plays when

    The player picks one track from the set for the time of day, never the same one twice in a row, plays it through, then leaves a quiet gap before the next. When the time of day changes (or you clock in or out), it waits for the end of the current section, at most 30 seconds, and cross-fades into the new set. Sleeping through the night changes it straight away.
