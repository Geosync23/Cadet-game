Wren Sound Lab

Everything new in one place: music for each time of day, and the sounds of doing things aboard. All of it is made live in your browser, with no recordings, and plays under the ship hum. Nothing here is in the game yet. The sounds already decided are on Wren Soundscape.

Tap a button to hear it. One piece of music plays at a time. Buttons with a ring around their dot are loops: tap once to start, again to stop. The volume sliders and a deck hum to play underneath are at the bottom. On an iPhone, if you hear nothing, check the silent switch.

Listen against the hum

Hum and volume

  • Every sound and every piece of music was measured against the Quarters hum and plays under it. Music sits well under it, about 7 to 12 dB quieter on average.
  • In activities, the only beeps are where a real device would make one: the scanner, the cart's charger, the airlock all-clear, the till and the phone ring.
  • The lo-fi pieces have a very quiet vinyl crackle. It's part of the style and easy to take out.
