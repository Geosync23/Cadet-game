How busy

People in the mess hall

The mess seats about 60. Every layer follows this: how often things clatter, how loud the galley is, and how many voices you hear.

In the game

How it would follow the real crowd

  • By who's there: the game counts the people in the mess hall, and every layer follows that count, as the steps above do. An empty mess makes no sound of its own.
  • Only in the room: it plays while you're in the mess hall and fades as you leave, like the galley and laundry sounds now.
  • Your tray and seat keep their own sounds on top, as now.
