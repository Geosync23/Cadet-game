Wren Soundscape

A first pass at how the ship could sound. Every sound here is made live in your browser, with no recordings, so it can be tuned by changing a few numbers. Tap any button to start; nothing plays on its own.

Headphones or a speaker help with the hum. Phone speakers can't play the deepest notes, so each hum also carries higher overtones you can still hear. On an iPhone, if you hear nothing, check the silent switch.

Ship hum

The Wren's background: engine, air and power, breathing slowly in and out. Each deck has its own feel. Tap one to hear it; tap another to cross-fade; tap again to stop.

Short and soft

Beeps and bloops

The device, the job board and the lift. Rounded tones, quick fades, nothing sharp. Where there are A, B and C, they're alternatives to compare.

Device tap

Plays for every button on your device. It will be heard hundreds of times, so it is the quietest sound here.

Open a panel

Two notes going up: the device, a machine panel or the job board opening.

Close a panel

The same two notes coming down, a little softer.

Job posted

A small marimba-like pair: something new on the board, no rush.

Job done

A warm four-note rise that rings out. The one sound meant to feel like a little reward.

Mail arrived

A says "message" in a playful way; B is calmer and would suit evening Mail about tomorrow's traditions.

Heads-up

For a tradition starting soon or a shift reminder. It should catch your ear without making you jump.

Not now / can't do that

A low, gentle "uh-uh" for a locked hatch or a full hand. Never a buzzer.

Weight and body · version 2

Doors

Rebuilt to sound like real machinery: a pneumatic release, a heavy panel rolling on its track, and a solid thump as it seals. No beeps or tones in any door. Three versions of each to compare.

Room doors

A · Pneumatic

A deep breath of air, then a firm slide and a solid stop. The classic starship door.

B · Heavy slide

Less air, more weight: you hear the panel rumble on its rollers and land with a deep thud.

C · Cabin

The same build, a size smaller and softer, for cabins and quiet rooms like the music room.

Hatches

A · Bulkhead

Two latch clacks, a slow heavy roll and a low metal clang. For machine rooms: you are going behind the walls.

B · Pressure hatch

A lever clunk and a big rush of air as the seal lets go. For the reactor and plant rooms.

C · Crawlway

A small hatch that rattles a little on its track, with a light metal knock. For crawlways and closets.

Archway (no door)

Optional. A faint change of air (just air, no tone) as you pass from one room to the next. We could also play nothing here.

Its own signature · version 2

The lift

The lift now sounds like nothing else on the ship: wide doors that part from the middle, a ride you can feel, a deep clunk as the car settles, and a struck-metal chime in its own key. Pick a lift, then play its parts or the whole trip.

Voices without words

Crowd

Made from simple voices that "talk" in vowel sounds, blurred so no words come through. Now and then someone laughs. The auditorium is a bigger, more echoing room than the mess.

Small is a table of three; medium is the mess at lunch; busy is the dinner rush or the hall before a show.

At walking pace

Footsteps

Your own steps, a little under two a second, each one slightly different so they never sound like a loop.

Plating for the junior wing and Deck 5, carpet for the officers' corridor, planks for the Deck 3 promenade, gravel for the garden.

    How it's built

    Choices so far

    • Everything is made live by the browser's own sound engine, so the game wouldn't need any sound files, and the hum never repeats.
    • The hum sets the loudness for everything else. Each sound was measured against it, so a beep or a door is never louder than the hum.
    • A gentle limiter sits on the master volume as a safety net, so stacked sounds can't spike.
    • The beeps share one family of notes so they sound like they belong to the same device. The lift chime is deliberately outside that family, in its own key and with a struck-metal tone, so you always know it's the lift.
    • Beeps only play when you do something. Nothing in the hum, the crowd, the doors or the footsteps is a beep.
    • Sound pauses when you switch away from the page, like the game does.
