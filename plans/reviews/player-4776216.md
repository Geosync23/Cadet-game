## MEDIUM: tapping the servery from across the room puts you in the line at once, and the counter waits for you
`playerTap` calls `galleyJoin('player')` at tap time, and galleyJoin calls `lineJoin` at once. The crew only join "on reaching its back" (section 10's own rule). In the probe you tapped from [19,10], 14 tiles away:
- you were at the **front of the line from 17:24 to 17:28 while still walking** across the mess (`act: queue, walk`, `k: 0`);
- `LINE_COUNTER` is 1 and `lineTick` skips 'player' (section 15 serves you), so **nobody behind you was served for those 4 ship minutes**: Torres, silva-bea and nandakumar stood behind someone who wasn't there yet;
- and from across the room you go ahead of crew already walking to the line.

In a rush, from the garden or a booth, that's a visible stall at the counter.
**Fix (player seat, with galley engineer 3, since galleyJoin is section 16's):** join on arrival, as the crew do. At the tap, walk to the line's current back spot (`lineSpotId(servLine().length)`, made with `placeSpot` or `lineSpotMake`) without joining; call `galleyJoin('player')` (and so `lineJoin`) when the walk ends (`actBegin` for `queue`); then follow `lineSpot` as now. If the back has moved on by the time you arrive, `lineSpot` walks you to your real spot, as it already does. Add it to player_check as a real-line case that runs only when `lineSpot` exists (today the line test only runs without it): tap from 10+ tiles away and check you're not in `servLine()` until you reach the back, and that someone ahead of you is served while you walk.

## Low
- **The tray never expires.** `SHIP.player.tray` is cleared only by eating it or by a New game. It survives sleeping (the `SIM_ADVANCE` hook ends other activities but leaves the tray), the lift and other decks. So you can take dinner at 17:30, sleep, and eat it as breakfast, and in the morning the servery says "You already have your tray". Clear it in the sleep hook and when you leave Deck 3 (you'd have put it in the return), or after the meal's serving window.
- **`A.o.dish` can be null at "Your turn"** (galleyTurn true, then `foodFrom` not ok in the same tick): actEnd sets `tray = null` and toasts "You take a tray: null." Guard: no dish, say `A.o.why` and end without a tray.
- **Lying down before 20:00 never turns into sleep.** `o.sleep` is fixed at the tap, so if you lie down at 19:50 and it turns 20:00 you lie there all night awake. Check `bedtime()` each frame in `bunkSleep` rather than once.
- **`stripTick` rewrites the strip's DOM every frame while any need is low:** the pulse value is part of `STRIP_SIG`, so six elements' styles change every frame. Fine on desktop. On a phone, keep the pulse in a CSS animation on a class, or round the signature to about 10 steps.
