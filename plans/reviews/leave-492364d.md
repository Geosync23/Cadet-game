# Review: leave, missed shifts and being let go (claude/leave 492364d, 0.61.0-0.61.2)

Reviewer 1d, 2026-10-17. The career engineer's work (session_01862tURG1qXAVGj2sdUrVCw) on top of 0.60.4 (d449914): commits 169d514, d87c719 and 492364d.
Checked against design-doc on claude/lead-pillar1: "One fail state, and it is very hard to reach" and "Leave, missed shifts and being let go".

## Verdict: PASS WITH FIXES

The system does what the design asks, and nobody who tries gets hurt by it:
- A diligent player, or one who turns up and does even one job a day, is never summoned.
- Leave never counts against you.
- Being let go always comes after both talks.
- The save is small, New game after the end works, and the shared code doesn't call `R()` at load.

There are two fixes I'd want before it ships (findings 1 and 2: a false missed shift for players updating from an older save, and a summons booked on your leave day). The rest are lows and design calls for the lead.

## The brief's questions
- **Can a diligent or casual player be summoned or let go?**
  - Diligent: no.
  - Turning up late every day but doing one job a day: work stays at 50, no summons (probe2).
  - Turning up 40 minutes late every day and doing nothing at all: the Chief on day 31, and never the Captain, because work levels off at about 16 (finding 4, a design call).
  - Let go: impossible without both talks and three workdays under 5.
- **Can sick days or leave count against you?**
  - On reputation: no. A leave or sick day is a day off to `playerShiftOn`, and the day-end count skips it.
  - But a summons can be booked on your leave day (finding 2).
  - Sickness is only a hook so far (`careerSick`); see the notes for the sickness review.
- **Let go before both talks?** No:
  - `careerDayEnd` counts a day at the bottom only when `st >= 2`, no step is waiting, and the Captain's latest talk is over.
  - `careerDue` books the Captain only after the Chief's latest talk is over.
  - leave_check's (c) confirms the order.
- **Reload or an old save?**
  - Reload: fine. `careerClean` round-trips. `q`, `qd` and `fl` are saved, the seek asks live in `SHIP.social` (saved), and an ended save shows the card again.
  - **An old save loaded mid-morning gets a false missed shift** (finding 1).
- **New game after the end?** Yes. The card's button (test URL, `NOSAVE`) gives Day 1 06:00, `SHIP.career` null, the card hidden, and the next workday has a shift again. Section 18's New game clears the career occasions.
- **Save budgets?** Fine: about 120 bytes after 280 casual days, 169 bytes at the end of a let-go run. Lists are capped at load. `c.b` isn't capped in memory, but you can hold at most 5 days of leave.

## Findings

### 1. MEDIUM: an older save loaded mid-morning gets a false missed shift
`careerInit` (starship-cadet.html ~27040) creates the career state on an old save with `on: 0`. If the player had already started that day on the older build (`flag('shiftSent')` is today and the `shiftStart` note was good), but is no longer in a Works room, 12:30 counts it as missed: work -15 and the "missed" warning.

Probe: day 10, started at 08:00 on the old build, save loaded at 11:00 on Deck 4. At 12:30 work goes from 50 to 35, `k` is 2 (the missed tip shown), and `on` is 0.

This hits a diligent player on their first morning with the new build. That's exactly the "diligent player never gets hurt" case.

**Fix (one line in `careerInit`):** `on: typeof flag === 'function' && flag('shiftSent') === d ? d : 0`.
- `shiftSent` is also set by the 10:00 "missed" note, so this can let a real no-show off once, on the day of the upgrade only. That's harmless.
- Alternatively, on an old save start with `e: d` (no miss counted for today).

### 2. MEDIUM (design): a summons can be booked on your leave day
`careerDue` books a waiting step on any day its person (Calder or Marrow) is on shift. It doesn't check whether you are.

Probe: work falls under 25 at 21:41 on day 15, and you book day 16 as leave. The Chief's talk lands on day 16 at 15:30, on your leave day.

What happens next:
- If you don't come in, the talk counts as held (missed), and the Captain's reprimand becomes possible.
- A missed occasion costs no reputation (section 19 skips `a.occ`).
- But you lose the warning talk, and the design's "plenty of warning" step goes by while you're on leave.

The same can happen on a weekend once `wait` (3 days) has run out.

**Fix:** in `careerDue`, also require `playerShiftOn(d)` (you're on shift that day). Keep the `wait` fallback, but only on your workdays.

### 3. LOW: Pell speaks at the send-off without being there, on most days
`careerSendoff`'s only appointment host is Torres (`occasionAdd` passes `with: [o.by]`). Pell and Calder come only through `gatherings`, and only if they're free.
- Pell's days off are Tuesday and Wednesday, so on the other five days Pell is at work on Deck 5.
- Pell still says three lines of the send-off, including the screw for your tin.
- leave_check prints this: in its run the last day is a Sunday, and "in their plans" lists silva-bea, teague-theo and calder, but not pell.
- Calder is free on Sundays, but not on a weekday last day.

**Fix:**
- Either let an occasion name extra people for its appointment: `with` on `OCCASIONS.careerSendoff` (`['pell', 'calder']`), and `occasionAdd` passes `[o.by, ...(o.with || [])]`. `apptWhere` already checks `a.with.includes(p.id)`, so section 14 walks them there and keeps them for the whole send-off.
- Or have the writers give Pell's lines a fallback speaker.

### 4. LOW (design call: the lead keeps it as is, 17:25): turning up late every day and doing nothing reaches the Chief
Being 40 minutes late on every workday, with no jobs, never missing a shift: work goes 41, 34, 29, 25, 23... by Friday of each week and levels off at about 16. The Chief's talk comes on day 31; the Captain never comes (probe2).

This comes from section 20's existing -2 for being late. The career code only adds the talk on top. A talk isn't a fail, and "turns up and does nothing" is close to not trying, so I think this is acceptable. But the design says "being a bit late is a small dip", so the lead may want the Chief's talk to need at least one missed shift (`SHIP.rep.cnt.missed > 0`) as well.

### 5. LOW: leave_check's late days don't actually dip reputation
leave_check sends `simNote('shiftStart', { late: 40 })`. Section 20 only scores that note in its social hour (`socHear` queues it, and `socialHour` applies it). The check never runs a social hour, so the casual player's late days, and the skipper's 10:00 "missed" notes, cost nothing in the check.

Its (a) casual case is therefore kinder than real play. The real numbers are still fine: probe2 calls `repNote` directly. The skipper's ladder would run a little faster in real play.

**Fix:** in `PAPER`, call `repNote('shiftStart', ...)` next to the `simNote` (as probe2 does), or run `socialHour` for the hour.

### 6. LOW (latent): the Chief's step can be overwritten by the Captain's before it is booked
In `careerLadder` (~27116), `c.st === 1 && w < 12` sets `q = 2` even while `q === 1` (the Chief's talk not yet booked). `careerDue` then waits forever for a Chief's talk that never happened:
- No summons.
- `n` never counts, so no let-go either.
- Only recovering to 25 clears it.

Probe: due at 16:01, then work under 12 before 07:00. The result is `st: 2, q: 2` for 45 days, with no steps and no occasions.

In today's rotas I can't reach it in play:
- Calder works every day you work.
- A miss near 25 only takes about 7.
- Weekends pull work up.

But a future change to Calder's rota, leave or sickness, or a bigger hit, would open it.

**Fix:** step up only when nothing is waiting: `if (!c.q && (c.st === 0 && w < CAREER.chief || c.st === 1 && w < CAREER.captain))`.

### 7. LOW: a cancelled leave day can be lost at the cap
If you hold 5, book a day (4 left), earn a grant (back to 5) and then cancel, `min(max, l + 1)` leaves you at 5. That's one day lost, which is rare and arguably fine. An alternative is to stop the grant from going over the cap while a booked day is still in the future. Mention it in the leave tip if you keep it.
