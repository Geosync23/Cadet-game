The arrival evening

The two scenes of your first evening aboard, as they play in the dialogue panel. You walk yourself the whole way; these open when you reach the place.

At your bunk (17:53, Day 0)

  1. Torres: Bunk 1. Home. Top bunk at the back is yours.
  2. Torres: Your locker's under it. Bag in there. Go on, it's yours now.
  3. Tomas: Oh! You're here! Hi. I'm Tomas. Watch. Bottom bunk, by the door.
  4. Tomas: I only got here last Sunday. So I'm not the newest any more. Sorry. I'm very pleased about that.
  5. Torres: Tomas will show you the washroom in the morning. Won't you, Tomas.
  6. Tomas: Yes! It's left at the end. It's left. It's probably left.
  7. Torres: And this is yours too. Your device. Mail, your schedule, maps, the lot.
  8. Torres: It'll want to say hello. They always do. Let it.
  9. You choose one:
    • You: What do I do first?
      Torres: Dinner. Always dinner first. Then everything else.
    • You: Is it always this much?
      Torres: First night, yes. By Thursday it's just home with a lift in it.
    • You: (Just nod)
      Torres: Good. The quiet ones last longest.
  10. Torres: Right, onward. The mess is on Deck 3, and Brennan doesn't hold plates for anyone.

Dinner: the Chief stops by (about 18:30)

  1. Torres: Chief. Didn't think we'd see you tonight.
  2. Calder: You haven't. I'm not here. Day off.
  3. Calder: Calder. Works is mine. Welcome aboard.
  4. Calder: Tomorrow you're with Torres. Ten to eight, the Works bay. Not eight. Ten to.
  5. Calder: Tuesday, your first job's mine. I'll pick it. You'll do it.
  6. You choose one:
    • You: Looking forward to it.
      Calder: Good.
    • You: What should I read up on?
      Calder: The scrubber manual. Chapter three. Nobody reads chapter three.
    • You: (Say nothing)
      Calder: Quiet. Good. The ship's loud enough.
  7. Calder: One thing. Nobody thanks the air. That's how you know it's working.
  8. Torres: She tells everyone that.
  9. Calder: It's true for everyone.
  10. Calder: Eat something. That's an order, and I'm not even here.
