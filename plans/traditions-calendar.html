Traditions calendar

Pillar 4, check-in A: the ship's traditions as the three designers drafted them, for you to keep, cut or add to. 21 new ones plus the twelve already aboard. Built from the files in content/traditions/ (claude/ship-events).

Your choices

  1. Keep, cut or add. Everything below is in the game already (it runs on paper and passes the checks); anything you cut simply goes.
  2. Garden hour clashes with the Monday evening set (book circle, talent night, department cup), so it rarely happens. The weekly designer is moving it, probably to Saturday afternoon.
  3. Shared meals (Sunday long table, the tasting table, the crossover supper, the suppers, long breakfast) work, but few people sit down yet. The sim engineer is making going count as that person's dinner or breakfast.
  4. Not yet built: the props most traditions ask for (only Tiles boards, the tasting chalkboard, the lanterns and your seedling exist), the votes (tasting, department cup, harvest best) and the Sunday "This week aboard" Mail. The farewell supper and crew welcome tea wait for crew coming and going.

The rule that keeps the ship from getting noisy: at most two traditions an evening and never two at once on the same deck; when they meet, the rarer one wins (so a monthly beats a weekly, a yearly beats both). Birthdays never move.
