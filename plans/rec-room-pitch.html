Flight simulator bay, in the rec utility room

You picked idea 4, the flight simulator bay, built in the rec utility room. The room's machines are kept elsewhere on the deck (the room can be made smaller, whatever works), and the float room stays as it is. FX14 plans and builds it.

The room today

Across the concourse from the pool is the float room: a padded room in coral and pink with drifting light orbs, where the gravity plates are turned down to about a fifth of normal. You walk in and float, which gives leisure and a little fitness. Floating is one of the six rec habits crew can pick up, and crew drop in now and then without one. It is 8 by 6 tiles, with a glass wall onto the lift lobby, so everyone walking past can see in.

The mechanical room

You asked whether the mechanical room on the rec deck matters for the career, and whether it could be the activity room instead. The rec deck has four machine rooms, all Works-only, behind hatches. I checked each one in the built game (0.92.2).

Rec utility room (the likely one)6 by 7 tiles, across the concourse from the pool's bow end, between the washroom and the auditorium. Holds the stage lights, the sound rack and the gravity plates for the float room, plus the lighting desk, the projector and the booth window onto the stage.
Pool plantA narrow room at the pool's bow end: two pool filters, the dosing unit and the heat exchanger.
River pump roomAt the aft end of the concourse: the two river pumps.
Emitter roomBeside the Lightdisc court: the court's net and its scoreboard.

Does the career need the rec utility room?

No. No career lesson, training or promotion step uses it. Its three machines only make ordinary jobs on the board (stage lights at Tier 1, the sound rack at Tier 2, the gravity plates at Tier 3), and when they wear, the auditorium's lights dim or flicker, the microphone fails, or the float room closes. The one rec deck story that is part of the Works career, tracing the leak (a drip in main storage on Deck 5 that you follow up the ladder to a pool filter), lives in the pool plant, and Chief Calder mentions that filter in a talk. So the pool plant should stay as it is.

Could the rec utility room become the activity room?

Yes, with one catch. The booth has to stay next to the auditorium: the lighting desk and the projector work through its window onto the stage, and on talent night Maeve Volkov sits in the booth to run the show. The simplest version keeps a narrow booth strip along the auditorium wall, with the three machines in it, still repairable as now, and turns the rest of the room (about 5 by 7 tiles) into the activity room with a door from the foyer.

  • Kept: every job and machine, the booth and talent night, the leak story, and the float room itself.
  • Moves: the machines shift a few tiles into the booth strip; the kettle and first-aid locker go with them.
  • New work: the room is rebuilt inside, a hatch becomes a door, and the deck checks are run again.

The upside of this option is that you get a new activity and keep the float room. Any of the six ideas could go in either room, though bowling needs the float room's longer 8 tiles.

Six ideas

Draw calls are the phone's main cost for a room. They are rough guesses from similar rooms already built, and would be measured before anything ships. "Small" is about one builder turn; "medium" two or three; "large" more, or a new mini-game.

Recommended

1. Table tennis and darts

Two table tennis tables and a dartboard wall. Tap a table and a free crew member comes over to play you, the way Lightdisc's "ask someone to play" already works; a rally plays out and you see who won. A chalk "winner stays on" board by the door.

Who comes
Lightdisc players and juniors after shift; a weekly ping-pong ladder evening; crew drop in between other rec turns.
What it adds
Leisure and social (more with friends), a little fitness. A quick game with a person, which the deck doesn't have yet.
Build
Medium. Tables and paddles are new kit; the "ask someone" flow exists.

What it adds
Leisure and energy rest, social from sitting near people. Pairs naturally with the pool and changing rooms.

Recommendation

Table tennis and darts

It is the one idea that adds something the deck doesn't have: a quick game with another person, in a room everyone can watch through the glass. It reuses Lightdisc's "ask someone to play" flow, it reads clearly on a small screen, and its cost sits in the middle. If you'd rather keep it cheap and calm, the steam room is the best second choice, since it sits right across from the pool and the changing rooms.

If the new activity goes in the float room, floating would be parked, not deleted: its code, its lines and the floater habit stay in the game, switched off, and the gravity plates would go with it unless you want them kept. If it goes in the rec utility room instead (see "The mechanical room" above), the float room stays as it is.
