In plain words

  • A new button on a person's chat card: Ask about the fault. They say where they were, when, and what they noticed ("It rattled when I went to bed, about 22:00").
  • What people noticed at a time crosses out the causes that couldn't show then. Nobody lies, but someone may be mixed up: their schedule (the Roster) had them somewhere else, so they mean a different machine. Spotting that is part of it.
  • The Chief's notes, new page: "Ask who was there, and check they were."

How witnesses work

  • Who knows: crew whose real plan had them near the machine (same room or the next) during the fault's window; they're found by Roster as now.
  • What they say: at the time they were there, the machine was showing or not (from the cause's when); "showing at t" crosses out causes that can't show at t; "quiet at t" crosses out causes that would show at t.
  • A mix-up is a person whose plan had them elsewhere; their line is about the machine where they really were. It crosses out nothing, and the case must be solvable without it.
  • Only once it unlocks (the user, 2026-10-18): until Tomas has had his word with you (Day 15's breakfast, or the next time you're both there if you miss it), there is no P2 case, no Ask button on anyone's chat card, and no witness line anywhere. A head-scratcher before then is one of the kinds you already have.

How it grows (bands)

  • T2: 3-4 suspects, 2-3 witnesses plus at most one mix-up, one deck.
  • T2+: a witness on another shift (find them on the Roster, maybe at night), witnesses on two decks.
  • T3: two stories both true, pointing to two faults.
