In plain words

  • Already in 0.60.7: every Wednesday at 10:00 the delivery lands in the cargo hold as crates; a job waits for you until 14:00: take a hover cart, pull in up to four crates, and the shelves unload themselves (one or two trips).
  • New: the delivery also has a few crates for the rest of the ship (linen for the laundry, medical supplies for the infirmary, stock for the crew store, spares for the bridge, dry goods for the galley). After the shelves, a manifest run takes them to their stops: load them from the hold (tap each; the cart fills), ride the lift, drop each one where it goes.
  • A junior job (the job-by-rank map). As an Officer you sign off the manifests instead.

Day 3, Wednesday

  1. 08:00Yara mentions the delivery(0.60.7, unchanged.)
  2. 10:00 onwardYou unload the parts crates(0.60.7.) It's offered, never forced.
  3. When the shelves are doneYara: "Now the rest of it"She meets you at the hold with the manifest: three crates today (linen, medical, store stock). She loads the first with you watching; you load the others and drive. She walks along to the first stop (the laundry, Deck 4), then lets you do the infirmary and the store on your own ("I'll see you back at the counter").
  4. AfterTrained"Trained: hauling, with Yara." Every Wednesday from now, the manifest run follows the unload, held for you till 16:00.

If you didn't take the unload, the crew did; Yara finds you after lunch with the manifest crates anyway. The drill at 15:00 is the day's small new thing (M3), so the run fits between: about 40 ship minutes.
