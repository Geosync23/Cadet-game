A fault that only shows at some times. Machines' logs show how hard they worked each hour; the pattern tells the causes apart. A small new thing on Day 12 (the day of tracing), on the case engine (case-engine.html), seat H. Plan Part 4, P1.

In plain words

  • "Showers weak on Deck 4, but only in the evening." A fault that comes and goes.
  • Each machine on the way keeps a log at its panel: how it ran each hour of the day. Read the logs: a cause that would be bad all day can't be it if the log is fine in the morning; one that only fails under the evening load can.
  • The Chief's notes, new page: "Bad all day: something's worn. Bad at the busy hours: something can't keep up."

On the device: the day strip

The case shows a day strip (00 to 24) per machine you've read: a small bar per hour of how it ran, your clues pinned at their times. Tap only; bars at least 12 px wide; scrolls sideways on a phone.

How it grows (bands)

  • T2 (Officer): one line, 4 suspects, 3 clues, logs only.
  • T2+: two machines share the time (the oven cool at dinner because the range and extractor pull on the same junction: the cause is a load, and the answer is "book it for the senior techs", a report, not a part).
  • With the logger (Step 5, a tool): clip it on, come back tomorrow, read a full day's trace where there's no log.
  • Tier 4: a drive coil that only stutters at full drive.

For the builder (seat H, the P1 kind in 24k)

  • CASE_KINDS.p1 = { ladder: 'p1', fits: causes of the kind (or of the machines on its MACHINE_LINKS line) that differ in when (always vs peak / after-use / time), suspects: the cause + 2-3 decoys with a different when, clues: one log per decoy machine (or per decoy cause on the same machine) }.
  • Logs are real: a log is the machine's hour-by-hour machWorks for the day before (from MACH_HIST), shaped by the cause: a peak cause drops only in the hours when its use counter is in its top quarter (machUseDay, normalised by machNorm), an always cause drops every hour. A decoy's log is its own true log, which is fine at peak, so it kills that decoy.
  • The ladder: row p1 (Day 12, small, Officer, by: ['calder', 'halloran-emeka']); its first case is the training case on the water line with cause 3-pump-1 at peak (fault set at 16:00 Day 11, posted Day 12 after the tracing lesson); if 3-pump-1 is busy, the first real-fault P1 that fits.
  • The day strip is a new device block { kind: 'strip', rows: [{ label, hours: [0-1 × 24], pins: [hour] }] }, built by H with a list fallback ("Fine 06-18, low 18-22").
