M6 · Making it safe: small incidents (Day 8, Calder) spec for approval

About once a week, a small incident from the machines: an hour or two on one deck. You make it safe, clean up, then fix the cause. Not a ship-wide event (those are Pillar 6). Step 2, sub-block 24f. Plan Part 2, M6.

In plain words

  • Three steps, always in this order: make it safe (isolate it: close the right valve or breaker, upstream, picked from what you see), clean up (mop the water, clear the smoke, help someone out), then fix the cause (an ordinary repair).
  • It's urgent but kind: a toast and the board's top card ("! Burst pipe, Deck 4 junior wing"); crew reroute around it and talk about it; nobody gets hurt; there's no timer. If you're not on shift or don't come, the crew deal with it (more slowly), and you hear about it at dinner.
  • Never on an event day (Pillar 6), on a tradition evening, at night (except your own two night shifts, Step 5), on a day off, or in your first week.

The three incidents (more can be added later as data)

A burst pipeA corridor floor is wet and crew step round it. Safe: close the line's valve (two or three valves on the nearby panel; the right one is the one upstream of the wet, following the pipe). Clean: mop the puddle (a few holds). Fix: replace the burst joint (one seal from stores, fitted at the burst).Decks 3, 4, 5
The lift sticksThe main lift stops between decks with someone inside, for up to 20 minutes; crew take the ladder (rides are slow). Safe: at the lift machine room on Deck 5, release the brake (a turn). Clean: help them out at the next deck (a short word; they're grateful for days). Fix: service the lift.the lift
The galley alarmA scorched pan sets off the extractor's alarm. Safe: reset the alarm at the extractor's panel (a hold). Clean: clear the air: open the vents and wait a few minutes. Fix: check the hood (look) and service the extractor. Brennan is embarrassed.Deck 3

When they happen

  • About one a week, on a workday between 09:00 and 15:30, from Day 8. Which incident, which day and where are worked out from the week (the same every game).
  • Your first is fixed: Day 8, Monday, about 10:30 (after the Monday briefing), a burst pipe on Deck 4.
  • They come from the machines: a burst pipe is more likely where the pipe valves are worn, the galley alarm when the extractor is, the lift sticking when the lift is.

Day 8, Monday: your first, with Calder

  1. 10:30The callAn urgent toast: "Calder: Burst pipe, junior wing. With me." She meets you at the Works bay door (or comes to find you, the escort's walking).
  2. SafeShe asks, you pickAt the wet corridor: "Which valve? Follow the pipe." You choose; if you pick a downstream one she says why not, and you try again.
  3. CleanMopShe hands you the mop. Crew step round you; Tomas makes a joke.
  4. FixThe jointFetch a seal and fit the joint, Calder watching. "Trained: making it safe, with Calder."

Day 8's small new thing is your work uniform (M2); Calder's "Tomorrow, your test job" line after the briefing is unchanged.
