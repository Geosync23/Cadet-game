In plain words

  • The verb: a team job is posted for a time ("Recycler membrane change, 09:30, Deck 3, a team of four"). You go to your spot when it starts; the lead calls each step; when it's your turn you hold your part (brace the housing, pass the tools, turn the crank) and the job moves on. The crew talk while they work.
  • It waits for you a little: up to 15 minutes at the start; if you don't come, they start without you and finish a bit slower. Never a fail.
  • Officer and up (the job-by-rank map). Leading one is M24, much later.

Day 11, Thursday morning (the ceremony is that evening)

Mail at 08:05 from Emeka Halloran, a Works systems officer: "Recycler's membrane bank, 09:30, Deck 3. Need four. You're the fourth. — E.H." At the recycler, Halloran gives each person a spot; yours is the housing brace. Three steps call on you: brace (a hold), pass the new cartridge (a carry from the crate to Halloran), brace again while it seats. About 40 ship minutes. "Trained: team jobs, with Halloran."

Torres is off on Thursdays and Fridays; Halloran is the Works officer on shift (by-list ['torres-zeke', 'halloran-emeka', 'calder'], first on shift).

The rota: two or three a week (the user, 2026-10-18)

  • Tuesdays and Thursdays, every week, at 09:30; and Fridays at 14:00 in even ship weeks (weeks 2, 4, ...: Days 12, 26, ...). So two one week, three the next.
  • The first is Day 11 (Thursday, week 2: the recycler, the training). Week 2's Friday (Day 12) is skipped, because it's your tracing lesson; so the rota really starts on Day 16 (Tuesday).
  • A day with an incident, an event, or your test job moves that day's team job to the next rota-free workday that week, or drops it. Never the same machine twice in one week. On your night shift (Days 19-20) they go ahead without you; you're not asked.
  • Posted the day before at 16:00, with Mail, as before (a big machine's overhaul or its planned service). A case's answer can be "needs a team job" (later bands); there's never a puzzle during one.
