In plain words

  • A machine doesn't just "break": something in it goes. An oven runs cool because its element is failing, or because its door seal lets the heat out. The job card says what everyone notices ("runs cool"); up close you can tell more ("cool all over" or "cool by the door").
  • From Day 2 every real fault has one of these causes. Before the scanner, inspecting tells you the part, as now. With the scanner you read the parts and choose. In a head-scratcher, the other causes become the suspects.
  • A few causes are settings (fixed by tuning, M5), and a few are loads (the machine is fine; something else is pulling on it), which only come up as head-scratchers from your Officer days.
  • Every part named here is already on a shelf in main storage. Nothing new to stock.

How to read the tables

  • How common (w): ●●● often, ●● sometimes, ● rarely. A worn part makes its cause likelier still.
  • Card (shows): the symptom on the job card. Causes that share it can be suspects together (P3 needs at least three with the same card).
  • Up close (sign): what you see at the machine, on rounds or the look-over. Causes with the same card have different signs, so when the scanner shows two low parts, the sign tells them apart.
  • When: always, at peak (its busy hours), warm (after it's been on a while), after use, touched (when someone uses the control), or at a time.
  • Still: the machine's line when you fit the wrong part.
  • From: the first band it can appear in (Tier 1 machines in T1 from Day 2; "Officer" means only in Tier 2 bands and later).

  • Texts (shows, sign, still) are keys into the Works writer's content/works/faults.lines; the plain words above are the first draft of those lines.
  • The pick (foundation 17.4): only causes allowed at the machine's band and day (from: a setting from Day 4; band), weighted by w × (1 + the part's wear share); a load is never picked as a real fault's cause on its own (it only appears in head-scratchers that say so).
  • Same card, different signs: within a kind, causes sharing a shows must have different signs (the scanner relies on it: when two parts read low, the sign tells them apart).
  • Parts: every part here is in STOCK today; compressor has only 2 (fine: rare) and signal card 2.
  • Corrections to the plan: the plan's Tier 1 list named the power junction and the bay door gear; in the game they're Tier 3 (junction) and Tier 2 (doorgear), so the junction appears only as a load suspect (P1) and the door gear is in the Tier 2 list. The Tier 1 kinds are the game's: oven, range, galley plant, extractor, chiller, washer, lift, light relays, cart dock, grow lights.
