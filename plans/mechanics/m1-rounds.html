In plain words

  • The verb: walk a route of 4-6 machines and look at each one (stand by it a moment; your character looks it over). You see a sign: "clean and quiet", "a faint rattle", "warm to the touch", "a drip under the pump". Then you mark it: fine, worn, or something's off.
  • Why it matters: a worn machine you mark gets a quick service job "found by you" (20 minutes, one part) instead of turning into a repair later. Something that's really going wrong gets its repair posted at once. That's prevention you can feel.
  • No wrong answers that hurt: mark a worn machine "fine" and nothing happens; the crew find it later, as now. Mark a fine one "worn" and Torres says "Looks all right to me." You get better at reading the signs; the Chief's notes and Torres's lines help.
  • Where it lives: a round clipboard (a Works datapad in your hand while you walk; the device shows the route). One round a morning, on the board as "Morning round, Deck 4".

  2. 10:30, the Works tea cornerTorres finds you"Fancy a walk? I'll show you my round." (A toast and the goal arrow; if you're busy, he waits for you at the Works bay until 11:30, then asks again after lunch.)
  3. The first two stopsHe looks, you watchDeck 4's round: Torres stops at the first washer, looks it over and says what he sees ("Hear that? Bearing's going. Worn."), marks it. The same at the scrubber.
  4. The restYou look, he watchesHe hands you the clipboard. You look at the next two or three and mark them; he says a word at each ("Good eye" or "Mm. Listen again").
  5. AfterTrainedAny worn machine you marked is on the board, found by you. "Trained: rounds, with Torres." From Day 3 there's a morning round on the board each workday, held for you until 11:00.

Day 2 also has your first laundry after shift (0.60.7) and Rina's health check (14:00), so rounds is the day's only new thing.

What you see at each machine

A sign is chosen from the machine's real condition and kind; the same machine in the same state gives the same sign that day. Examples (the Works writer writes the full set, a few per kind and state):

StateWasherOvenLight relaysAir scrubber
healthy"Drum turns clean and quiet.""Even heat, door seal snug.""Steady light.""A low, even hum."
worn"A faint rattle at the end of the spin.""The door seal's a bit tired.""A click now and then in the box.""The hum's a touch higher than it should be."
faulty and worse"Water pooling under it.""Hot one side, cool the other.""The lights stutter.""It's labouring; the air's stale near it."

The marks you can give are the same three everywhere. A worn machine is the one rounds are for: "something's off" on a faulty one also counts as found by you.

As you rise: as an Officer you choose any route (any deck you can enter); as a Senior Officer you also set the juniors' routes (the plan's job-by-rank map; built with Step 5).

The crew still do rounds

Today's crew inspection round (the round chore, 3 stops) keeps running. If you don't take your morning round by 11:00, it goes back to being a crew round, and they find worn machines as today. Nothing waits on you.

The job kind

jobKindAdd('rounds', { tier: 1, junior: false, work: 0, post: (h, jobs) => ... // workdays at 07:00, once trained (SHIP.works.tr.m1): one round, held for you to 11:00 stops: j => j.route.map(id => ({ deck, placeId: panelPlaceId(id), mins: 2, tag: 'look', step: 'look', opts: { marks: ['fine', 'worn', 'off'], sign: roundSign(id, day) } })), stepDone: (j, k, { pick }) => roundMark(j, k, pick), done: (j, t, by) => worksUse('m1'), crew: { can: p => p.work === 'work:works', mins: 25 } })
  • Route (stage 1): roundRoute(day): the deck by day % 3 (4, 3, 5; the training round is always Deck 4; skipping a deck with fewer than 4 machines with panel places), then 4-6 of its machines at your tier with panelPlace, ordered by a walk from the lift, picked with schedU(day, 0, 860). Stage 2: the player picks with a choose step at the first stop (up to 9 shown, 5 picked).
  • Signs: roundSign(id, day) = a line from signs.lines for the machine's kind and machineState now, varied with schedU(machSeed(id), day, 861).
  • Marks: roundMark: worn on a worn machine, or off on a faulty-or-worse one → machineFound(id, 'you'), xp +1 (foundation 24.4), the log's found-by-you flag. Anything else: nothing posted; a mark of worn/off on a healthy machine gets the teacher's (or the clipboard's) "Looks all right" line. Never a penalty.
  • Held for you: the round is held to 11:00 (j.until); then released to the crew (as CHORES.round does today). It never counts against HOLD_MAX.
  • Training (ladder row m1, foundation 24.2): Day 2, the ask at 10:35 (after the 10:30 tea); the teacher's round is the same kind with j.train = 'm1', stops 1-2 done by Torres (worksEscort lead, he stands at the panel, his line plays), stops 3+ yours (follow).
  • Stage 3: once hasTool('scanner'), a stop's place may be the room's doorway (roomDoorPlace, or the nearest walkable tile inside the door) covering every machine in that room (one look, one mark each).
