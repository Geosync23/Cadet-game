Lead's calls, for the user's morning review

Where the plan was vague, the simplest thing that fits:

  • Tool upkeep's lesson: Torres is off on Thursdays and Fridays, so whoever's on shift teaches it, the first time you use your bench.
  • Irrigation: the first harvest Saturday on or after Day 13.
  • Rewiring starts Day 17 (the weekend and the second test days come first).
  • The lift roof is Thursdays 15:30-16:30 (not mid-morning: team jobs and tea), and crew wait for the lift for the hour rather than climb the ladder.
  • The stocktake is Thursdays 13:30, first on Day 18.
  • Favour cases and tickets are built by E2 from H's handover (no H seat tonight); H reviews later.
  • Cabin touches and star logging had no seat: E3, in a new sub-block 24t.
  • Tier 2 reputation keeps C2's numbers; side jobs give at most +2 a day so they never rush a promotion.
  • The workshop's machines are plain places for now, not machines that wear.

Check-in 4: "a weekend of side jobs"

Start on Day 12 (Friday, an Officer): an order on the shop board, your bench, the log at 16:30. Days 13-14 (the weekend): a favour case, Calder's ticket Mail, the harvest's nozzles (if it's a harvest Saturday), stars at night, tinkering, your cabin. Then Days 17-18 for the weekday ones: rewiring, the stocktake, the lift roof.
