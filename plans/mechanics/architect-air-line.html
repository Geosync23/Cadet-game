In plain words

  • Air on the Wren runs in one line: the electrolyser makes oxygen, the oxygen cells hold it, the two main scrubber stacks (the air plants) clean the stale air, the main fan pushes it under the main corridor and up the spine, and two duct dampers split it to the upper decks (1-3) and the lower decks (4-6). Each deck's own scrubbers are at the far end.
  • When the card says "Stuffy air on Deck 4", you clip the pipe kit on in the middle first: the main fan. Then halve the line, as with water and heat.
  • Today the fan is behind glass with no way in, and there are no dampers. So: open one hatch from the air plant into the Fan hall, make the fan a real machine with a panel you can reach, and add the two dampers in the Fan hall where the duct leaves it.

Six steps with a real middle: reading the fan first splits the line in two, the way Calder teaches. Decks 1, 3 and 6 have no scrubbers of their own; their stuffy-air card ends at their damper, which is enough (the reading after the damper is the deck's air). No other duct machines are needed.
