In plain words

  • About one a week (a weekday, posted at 08:00): a worn valve starts to drip; the job says only "A pipe drips somewhere on Deck 4".
  • Find it: a puddle on the floor (M6's decal) and a damp patch on the wall show the room. Tap the puddle: "It's coming from above, towards the door" (a look), and walk that way to the joint. Sound comes later: then the drip gets louder as you close in.
  • Fix it: at the joint, tighten it (turn), or if it's worn through, replace it (a joint from stores).
  • Taught: the Works officer on shift (by-list Torres, Halloran) finds the first with you: "Follow the wet, not the noise. Well, both."
  • Not taken by the end of your shift: the crew find it the next day the long way. You get: "found by you", Works reputation +1.
