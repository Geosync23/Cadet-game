In plain words

  • The order board in Deck 5's machine shop gets one or two orders a week (posted Mondays 08:00) from crew you know: "A hook for my guitar, please. Pell." Each order names a thing and a person.
  • Make it: take the order (tap the board), then at the lathe or the fabricator a wait (10 ship minutes) and a short hold to finish. The thing goes into a hand.
  • Hand it over in person (tap them): a thank-you line and a friendship step. An order you never take lapses after 7 days ("I made do, don't worry"): no harm.
  • Tinkering (from Day 6, off shift): at the same machines, make one thing from a short list (a lamp, a mug rack, a toy rocket, a plant pot) for a gift (Pillar 4's gifts) or for your cabin (cabin touches). Scrap is free; one at a time.
  • Who: the machine shop tech on shift teaches the first order (a by-list, first on shift). You get: Works reputation +1 per order handed over (at most one a day), a friendship step, and leisure while tinkering.
