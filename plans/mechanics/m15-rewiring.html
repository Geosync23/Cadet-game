Lead's call, for the user's morning review: the first rewiring is Day 17 (Days 13-14 are a weekend and 15-16 the second test days).

In plain words

  • About once a week (posted 08:00 on a weekday): "Rewire junction 4-J2."
  • What: open the box; the device shows the colour chart (4 to 6 wires). Choose a wire, then its terminal (choose steps), until all are in; close up and test.
  • A wrong pair: a fuse pops at the test; open it and redo only that pair. "Right first time" if none.
  • Taught: Calder, on an officers' corridor junction, on the first one (Day 17, Wednesday morning). You get: Works reputation +1 (+1 more right first time).
