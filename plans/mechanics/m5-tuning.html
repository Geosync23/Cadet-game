In plain words

  • Machines drift: now and then an oven runs hot, a light relay chatters, a cart dock charges slow. It's on the board as "Tune: Oven 2 (runs hot)".
  • The verb: at the machine's panel, turn its setting one step at a time (two big buttons, − and +). After each step the machine answers on the deck: the glow calms or flares, the flicker slows or quickens. Settle it and it's done. Overshoot and it shows the other way; turn it back.
  • The crew tune too (today's "calibrate" chores become tuning), so a drifted machine never waits on you.

Settled (0): the job is done; turn-in as any job.
  • Overshoot: nothing special; the look shows the other side.
  • Stage 2 (scrubbers, Tier 2): two settings on the card; turning fan by 1 moves flow by ½ (rounded toward 0) and the other way round; done when both are 0. The look: fan high → a higher hum (later, with sound; today the scrubber's flicker), flow low → dim.
