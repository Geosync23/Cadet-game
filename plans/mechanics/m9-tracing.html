In plain words

  • The job card names the symptom, not the machine: "Showers weak on Deck 4", "Cold cabins, officers' corridor".
  • The verb: with the pipe kit (a new tool, with its lesson), clip it on at a machine's panel and read the flow there (a bar and a word: full, low, very low). Walk along the line reading machines: where the flow is fine before a machine and low after it, that machine is the cause. Then it's an ordinary repair.
  • The Chief's notes get a page: "Read the middle first. Halve the line each time."
  • Wrong turn costs a walk, never a fail. The board's card says which line it's on; the device's Maps app highlights the line's machines.
