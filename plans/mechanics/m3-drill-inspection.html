In plain words

  • Wednesday 15:00, the drill: a toast at 14:50; go to your muster station (the Works bay) and do your drill job. Calder times the Works team; a quick drill gets a word at Friday tea.
  • Thursday morning, the inspection: before 06:30, have a clean uniform, your berth made and your locker tidy. The duty officer walks the junior wing and gives each a nod or a gentle remark ("Uniform's seen better days, cadet").
  • Both count toward your Works reputation (turning up) and the crew's opinion. Missing one costs a remark, nothing more.

The drill

Fire drillTake the extinguisher from its bracket by the Works bay door and stand by the hatch until the all-clear.
Pressure drillClose and dog the bulkhead door by the Works bay: turn the wheel (a turn step, four turns) and stand by.
Power drillStand by the breaker cabinet in Deck 5's utility room; at the call, switch the junior wing to reserve (a hold), then back.
  • The kind rotates by week (fire, pressure, power), the same every game. The device's Calendar says which on Wednesday morning.
  • Your first (Day 3): Calder briefs you at 14:50 in the Works bay: "Fire drill. Extinguisher, hatch, wait for me." It's a training moment of one step: she watches you do it. "Trained: the drill."
  • Timing: Calder's line at the end, from how long the Works team took (you included): "Two minutes ten. Better." It's words, never a score.
  • Off duty? On a day off you muster by the lift like the off-duty crew (as today), no drill job.

The inspection

  • Three things: a clean uniform (0.60.7's morning change, and not grubby: M2 from Day 8), your berth made (tap your bunk and "Make the bed": a short hold, from 05:30), your locker tidy (nothing loose: things you bought or made put away; a tap on the locker "Tidy up", a short hold, puts them away).
  • Your first (Day 4): Pillar 4's inspection scene with Tomas panicking about his bunk becomes the real check: he shows you how ("Corners. She checks the corners."). It's the day's small new thing.
  • The walk: the duty officer (an officer of the day by rota; Calder on your first) walks the junior wing at 06:30; at your berth they look over all three and say one line. You don't have to be there (if you are, they speak to you; if not, the remark comes by Mail).
  • What it gives: all three right, a nod and +1 reputation (foundation 24.4); one or two wrong, a remark; none, a firmer remark. Never a fail.
  • No puzzles at the inspection (the plan's links).
