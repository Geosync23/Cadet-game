In plain words

When your Works reputation reaches "Relied on" and you've learned the Junior work (rounds, hauling, the drill and inspection, tuning, the scanner, your first incident), Calder sets your test job: a real Tier 2 job, with her watching. Pass it and the next work day you're summoned, promoted to Officer (Technician, Tier 2), cheered by the crew, walked to your own cabin, and the day after, commissioned by the Captain. Nothing about it can be missed, and the test job can only ever be delayed, never failed for good.

Day by day (a steady player)

  1. Day 8, Monday, after the briefing"Tomorrow, your test job."Calder, in person at the board (or by Mail if you're not there by 10:00). The Record's "What's next" line says it too.
  2. Day 9, TuesdayThe test jobA real Tier 2 job held for you: one of Deck 4's air scrubbers (4-scrubber-1 or -2), with Calder following. You use what you've learned: the morning round finds it ("something's off"), the scanner reads it (the filter is low), you fetch a filter and fit it, and a tuning step settles the fan. She says a word at each step. Torres is on Tuesdays and comes by at the end.
  3. Day 10, Wednesday, 10:30The summonsAn urgent toast, not a quiet badge: "Chief Calder: My office, 11:00. — C." A real appointment in her glass office in the Works bay; the goal arrow points there; Torres: "Go on. She doesn't bite. Usually." If you haven't come by 11:15, Calder walks over to find you wherever you are on the ship and says it there.
  4. 11:00, Calder's officeThe sceneShe tells you you've passed; hands you the Technician's badge and your Officer's collar pip; you choose one reply (proud, surprised, "about time"), which she remembers.
  5. AfterThe crewYou walk out into the Works bay and the Works crew on shift are by the board: applause, Torres's grin, "Told you" from Pell if he's on. All day, people who know you react when you meet; Mail from friends and the Captain; a news item in the Ship app; Brennan's cake at the Works tea corner after the shift.
  6. 17:00The moveCalder walks you to cabin 4-23 (below).
  7. Day 11, ThursdayThe ceremonyMail from the Captain's office in the morning; your first team job (M8, its own page) in the morning; at 17:15 the officer ceremony in Deck 1's observation room (below). Officers aren't inspected, so that morning's 06:30 inspection isn't yours.

The test job: only ever delayed

  • Passed when the scrubber is put right (the filter fitted and the fan settled), whatever it took. Wrong picks or a slow job don't fail it; they're mentioned kindly.
  • Delayed only if you don't do it (not taken by 15:00, or dropped): Calder says what to practise ("Get a few more scrubbers' worth of filters under your belt") and sets another in two work days. There's no limit and nobody is ever passed over.
  • It opens no earlier than Day 9, even for a very busy player, so the first week keeps its pace.

The move to 4-23 (Day 10, about 17:00)

  • Calder walks you there (Commander Vance if it's her day off): from the Works bay, up the lift, along the officers' corridor. On the way she talks about officers' ways: your door, quiet hours after 23:00, the "Come over" visits.
  • On the way you pass Tomas and Pell carrying your box.
  • At 4-23 the door plate already has your name and rank; your key opens it; your things are there: what was in your locker, the keepsakes on your shelf, gifts you were given. Your clothes and wardrobe move too (your laundry is still yours until the Deck 2 suite).
  • Tomas says goodbye as a bunkmate: "Don't be a stranger, Officer." That night you sleep in your own cabin, and Pillar 4's "Come over" visits start.
  • If you're busy at 17:00 (mid-job), Calder waits up to 30 minutes, then finds you; if you're asleep or off the ship's day plan, the move happens anyway and Calder's lines come by Mail.

The officer ceremony (Day 11, 17:15)

  • Where and who: the observation room on Deck 1 (the conference table, the stern windows): the Captain, Commander Vance, Calder, Torres, and up to three of the friends you're closest to (chosen by the game from your friendships).
  • Short and formal: the Captain reads your commission, pins your Officer's insignia (your uniform shows it from now on: a small collar mark, a colour change, nothing new drawn), a handshake, one line from you, a toast with the Captain's tea.
  • A clean uniform matters (M2): if you're grubby, Calder gives you a look and the Captain says nothing; nothing else changes.
  • A keepsake: the framed commission goes on your cabin shelf. Your Record: "Commissioned Officer, Day 11."
  • If you miss it (you don't come by 17:30), Calder comes to find you and walks you up; it can't be missed.

After the promotion

  • Your rank is Officer, your Works title Technician (Tier 2). The board starts leaving you Tier 2 jobs; junior jobs (the food cart, hauling runs) aren't held for you any more but stay on the board (the user, N4).
  • Your Works reputation starts again as "New officer".
  • Head-scratchers come more often (about 1 in 5 of your real repairs).
  • The Deck 5 machine rooms and the pipe galleries are now your work (tracing, M9, Day 12).
