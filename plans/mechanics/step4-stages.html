In plain words

  • Rounds stage 2 (after 5 rounds): the clipboard lists the deck's machines with "last looked" days; you pick 4 to 6. Finding two or more worn ones on a round earns a word from Torres and Works reputation +1.
  • Rounds stage 3 (with the scanner, from Day 5, after stage 2): from a room's doorway one scan reads every machine in it (one look; it costs the scanner's charge, M11).
  • Hauling stage 2 (after 2 manifest runs, Day 10 on): the manifest's stops come unordered; you choose the order; fewer lift rides makes "A tidy run".
  • Hauling stage 3, the stocktake (Thursdays 13:30, from Day 18): walk the storage aisles and count the shelves (a short hold each); one shelf is short; finding it posts a restock job before a repair is blocked.
  • Team jobs stage 2 (after 2 team jobs, from Day 18): the team job's Mail asks you to bring the parts: a manifest run from stores to the machine before the start. If you don't, the lead fetches them and it starts 10 minutes late. Never a fail.
