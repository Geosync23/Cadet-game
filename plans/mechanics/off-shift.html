In plain words

  • Cabin touches (once you have cabin 4-23, Days 10-11): four spots, the floor (a rug), the desk (a lamp), the shelf (keepsakes), a small ledge by the door (a plant). Tap a spot and pick from your things that fit it (bought at the crew store or made by tinkering). Things placed there count as put away (the locker's tidy).
  • What it does: with two or more touches, you wake a little happier (mood); visitors notice ("Nice lamp. Did you make that?").
  • Star logging (from Day 6, any evening 20:00-23:30): at Deck 2's observatory telescope, a short look: three marked points in the sky, one is new; pick it and log it (30-60 s). One a night.
  • What it does: Survey warms to you (a friendship step with Dr. Sato or the Deputy Survey Chief every three logs); after ten, a Record line, "Star logger"; on Day 19 the sensor array lesson says your logging paid off.
