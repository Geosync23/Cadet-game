Lead's call, for the user's morning review: is Day 13 a harvest Saturday? If harvest falls on Days 6, 20 ..., the first is Day 20. The first harvest on or after Day 13 either way.

In plain words

  • When: harvest morning (every other Saturday, the Pillar 4 tradition), while the crew pick.
  • What: walk the garden's three grow racks; a few nozzles on each are clogged (2 to 4 in all); at each rack a short hold per clogged nozzle clears it.
  • What it does: the next harvest is a little bigger (up to a tenth more), and that evening's salad gets a mention.
  • Taught: the gardener on the rota shows you the first rack. You get: a friendship step with the gardeners over a few harvests, Works reputation +1.
