In plain words

  • Twenty events, each once. Thirteen from the earlier plans, three new ones from the user's brainstorm (the distress call, the stowaway, reactor trouble) and four more the user picked, inspired by Star Trek: The Next Generation (Echo Signal, Loss of Power, the Visiting Inspector, Space Creature; "that will be enough": no others). Each is pitched in full on its own page.
  • Close together (the user: "events shouldn't be so far apart"): eleven of the twenty fall in Days 4-40, six of them inside the career arc (Days 1-26), the distress call among them. A game of 10-15 hours meets most of them.
  • Around the career's big days: nothing on a promotion test, a promotion or its ceremony, or Calder's question. Short cozy evenings sit on quiet career days.
  • Cozy and dangerous mixed (the user: "both"); the dangerous ones can hurt someone a little (a day or two in the infirmary), never worse.
  • You can't fail one. Every event ends; it goes smoothly, bumpily or roughly, told as story; something lasting stays on the ship either way.
  • Your part grows with your rank: as a junior you carry and fix, as an Officer you trace, as a Senior Officer you plan around people, as an Engine Specialist you lead a team with the bridge on the line.

The days are fixed, so every game tells the same story, and they follow the ship's calendar, not your progress. An event that needs someone (Calder for the storm, Vance for the visit, Sato for the comet and the echo) moves a day if they're off; the next one keeps its own day. Gaps: two to five days in Days 4-40, a week after.

How every event works (kept from the design doc)

  • Ambient: never a mission you're given. News reaches you as it reaches everyone: chatter, the Ship app's news feed (with who told you), Mail, the boards, and at big turns the Captain's address on every deck. No cutscenes; the camera stays with you.
  • Shape: warning, onset, escalation, resolution, afterglow, over one to four days.
  • Four kinds of moment: felt everywhere, the Captain's address, gatherings at a real place and time, and meetings with dialogue (a panel of lines, sometimes two or three replies; walk away any time, the gist comes by Mail).
  • Jobs by tier: each event's jobs go on the board tagged with the event; you see the ones for your tier and below. The Tier 3 and Tier 4 rows are slots: the kind of work is named here, and Steps 5 and 6 of Pillar 5 (shutdowns, the night shift's senior post, the Monday briefing, leading a team, the reactor) give them their exact verbs.
  • Threads and bands: three to five threads per event, measured from what really happened; each lands smoothly, bumpily or roughly, and the blend is the result. Your own part (jobs done, meetings, people helped) is counted separately: your commendation, the friendships, a mention in the debrief.
  • Traces: something stays on the ship either way: a pinned roster, a plaque, a sculpture, a pet, a scorch mark.
  • Traditions: paused in an event's hardest phases (no quiz night in a storm), except birthdays; a party or gathering after.

1. Ion storm main · danger · Days 4-6 (Thu-Sat), 3 days · built (0.34.12), to be changed

Survey spots an ion front ahead that the Wren can't steer round. The ship rides it out for two days: flickering lights, no calls home, a tired bridge. The first time you see the whole crew pull together.

  1. Day 4 Thu 06:00 · warningDr. Sato's readings; chatter starts in Survey. 08:15 Calder's storm briefing at the Works job board. 12:30 the first all-hands (the Captain at the lectern in the auditorium: "We're heading into weather").
  2. Day 4 Thu 14:00 · onsetFelt everywhere: a jolt, every deck drops to emergency lighting for about 20 minutes (the only emergency lighting of the storm, the user's rule), then flickering. The Captain's address. Comms down: booths shut, no outside Mail. Bridge on double shifts; the bow lounge closes.
  3. Day 5 Fri · escalationPower wear doubled, comms tripled: relay faults, the chiller strains (no more blackouts). Storm stew; trays to the bridge by cart. Crew short-tempered, sleep worse.
  4. Day 6 Sat 07:30 · resolutionThe all-clear in the observation room (a meeting) and on every deck. Comms back: a queue at the booths all morning. 09:00 Sowing morning goes ahead: the first calm morning. Evening: film night, for the first time in the auditorium, as the storm party.
  5. Day 7 Sun · afterglowChatter, the debrief Mail, commendations. Vance's bridge invitation (for bridge hour on Day 23) if you helped (see "The links"). Kovac is out of the infirmary after a day: she cracked her elbow in the jolt.
  • Dialogue: Calder's briefing (one choice: take the bridge battery run, the lounge, or nothing); the first all-hands; the all-clear (if you're there, the Captain thanks the Works crew by name).
  • News feed: "Sato hasn't left the chart room since last night." · "They say it's the size of a moon." · "No calls home till it's over." · after: "Lights only went once, thanks to Works."
  • Ship health: power and comms dip hard on Days 4-5; food if the chiller strains; mood dips, then lifts at the party.
  • Goes well: short flicker, nothing spoiled, a cheerful film night: the storm crew's signed duty roster pinned in the tea corner.
  • Goes poorly: long faults, binned food, a grumpy weekend: a scorch mark and a taped panel in the relay room.

8. Space Creature main · cozy (wonder) · Days 29-31 (Mon-Wed), 3 days · NEW (TNG-inspired)

The power keeps dipping and nobody can find a fault. Then the bow windows show a soft glow on the hull: a baby space creature has latched on near a power conduit and is feeding on the Wren's power. Nobody wants to hurt it. Works weans it off, little by little.

  • Goes poorly: it took longer: a scorched hull plate where it fed (a job for later); it still swims off safe.
