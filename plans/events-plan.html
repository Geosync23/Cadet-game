Ship-wide events: the plan

Events planner EP1, 2026-10-20, from build 0.64.2 (branch claude/events-plan). Planning only: nothing in the game has changed. Your scope: only the events up to the end of the career (the promotions). The rest are cut.
In short
  • When the career ends: on Day 26 (a Friday), with "Calder's question". It's the same day for a steady player and a busy one, because each promotion step has a first possible day and working harder can't bring it forward. Promotions: Officer on Day 10, Senior Officer on Day 16, Engine Specialist on Day 23. Today's game only has the first. The other two come in Pillar 5 steps 5 and 6, which are built before the events.
  • So three events are in scope: the ion storm (already built), comet viewing (a short one, drafted) and the diplomatic visit (mostly built, kept switched off). Everything else on the design list (rotation day, the garden creature, power surge, illness, Founders' Day, nebula, supply shuttle, salvage, the Veyli vessel, déjà vu) falls after Day 26 and is cut.
  • The ion storm is switched on today and it works. I ran it from start to finish (screenshots below): Calder's briefing, the jolt and the Captain's address, storm stew, blackouts, the all-clear and a full party in the bow lounge. But it was built before most of the game existed. It now lands on the same day as your first promotion. It also doesn't show on your device's Schedule. The crew's 89 storm lines never play. Calls home still work while comms are down. And it clashes with the rec deck and the Works career. That's 14 things to update, listed below.
  • Build order: a shared base built once, then the storm update, then the comet, then the diplomatic visit. Each one is built switched off, tested with Dev mode, then switched on. That's about 26-33 build turns in all.
  • I need your answer on 7 questions (at the bottom). Each has my recommended answer.

How I got Day 26, and the steady, busy and slow players
  • Built today (0.64.2): one promotion, Junior Technician to Officer (Tier 2). It needs "Relied on" reputation plus training in m6, which opens no earlier than Day 8. The test job is never before Day 9. The summons comes at 11:00 on Day 10, Calder walks you to cabin 4-23 at 17:00, and the ceremony is Day 11 at 17:15. I ran career_check on 0.64.2: the steady player passes the test on Day 9, is promoted on Day 10 and has the ceremony on Day 11. The busy player reaches "Relied on" a day earlier (Day 4, which the check now flags because it expects Day 5 or later: a small tuning drift worth telling the career seat about) but still gets promoted on Day 10. The gates are calendar days. Nothing in the code sets Tier 3 or 4 yet.
  • Planned (Pillar 5 plan v11, approved): Tier 3 test on Day 15 (Mon), Senior Officer on Day 16 (Tue), the Captain's table toast on Day 17, the night shift on the nights of Days 19 and 20, the Monday briefing and Tier 4 test on Day 22, Engine Specialist on Day 23, the reactor on Day 24, leading a team on Day 25, then mentoring and "Calder's question" on Day 26 (Fri). Its rule: "if you're slower, the next thing waits", so every date is the earliest possible. A busy player can't get there sooner.
  • A slow player (barely working) gets there later. By the gates, Officer comes around Day 25-28 and the end is well past Day 40. Events are tied to the calendar, not to your rank (question 4), so a slow player lives the storm as a Junior. That's what "what you see depends on rank" is for.
  • Two events fit in 27 days at the design's pace of "one main event every 1-2 ship weeks": the storm and the diplomatic visit, with the comet (the only short event slotted between them) in the gap.

2. The ion storm today

Is it on? Yes. It has been on since 0.34.12, with no switch other than the draft flag, which the diplomatic visit has and the storm doesn't. It starts by itself on Day 10 (the first day Calder's on shift from Day 10). Only the diplomatic visit is off. There's no way to start it by hand in the normal game. Tests do it through the test hook (eventStart('ionStorm', day)), and Dev mode will need to do the same.

What I saw when I ran it (phone size, 390 × 844, new game, clock skipped forward, me standing where each moment happens):

The result with me doing nothing: rough. That came from 405 minutes of blackout, 17 meals spoiled, the bridge hungry for 12 hours and a call-home backlog of 6. It left a scorch mark and a taped panel. An earlier run came out bumpy, so the result flips between runs. No commendation, even though I'd signed up for the battery run at the briefing: the crew did it first. No errors on the page.

What's built (the full list)

  • Phases: warning on day 0 at 06:00, onset on day 1 at 14:00, escalation on day 2 at 06:00, resolution on day 3 at 10:00 and afterglow from day 3 at 22:00 to the end of day 5. The resolution and afterglow put everything back to normal.

  • Machines: power, comms and chiller wear 1.5-3× faster. Rolling blackouts go one deck at a time (Decks 1, 4 and 5 were seen). Storm jobs go on the board: the lounge, the ballasts, battery packs to the bridge, the Tier 3 junctions, and a tidy-up in the afterglow.
  • Galley: storm stew, slower cooking, more grab-and-go, 6 trays to the bridge.
  • Crew: double shifts for command and helm, and the Watch patrols. The bow lounge and call booths close. 40-50% of the crew stay in, there's a rush on the booths on the all-clear day, and needs shift (energy refills slower, social drains faster).

    • The surge on day 2 at 11:20.
    • The Captain's address at the front, and again at the all-clear, worded by how it went.
    • Calder's briefing, a meeting with a choice of reply.
    • The Captain's all-clear, a meeting.
    • The party in the bow lounge, a gathering (37 crew planned).
  • Mail and news: Calder's warning, Vance's "Storm stations", the debrief worded by how it went, the commendation, and notes on any meeting you missed. 7 news items feed the social feed.
  • Traditions: paused at the onset and in the escalation (birthdays still happen).
  • After it: a commendation (two storm jobs, or one job and a meeting). That leads to the Captain's ready room, the Captain's table (Day 20, every 28 days) and the bridge invite (Day 22, waiting for the first event to end).
  • Checks: events_check parts 5-9 (on paper, live, occasions, hooks, the diplomatic visit), food_check 4h/4i, machine_check 4c, interactions_check, career_check (bridge invite).

  • Dev mode (the user's ask): it needs to (a) start any event now, even one still switched off, (b) jump to a chosen phase (front, escalation, all-clear, party), (c) end it and see the result, (d) set the rank, to see each rank's parts, and (e) move the clock. The game can already start an event in tests (eventStart), so Dev mode just needs a button for it. It must work on a build where the event is switched off, because that's how each event is checked before you switch it on.

Build the events after steps 5-6 (as the roadmap says), but agree F1 with the Pillar 5 seats first.
