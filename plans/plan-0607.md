# 0.60.7 plan: one more pass before Pillar 5

*The 0.60.7 planner (session_0165oGATedZqZNCLXnQWytMv), 2026-10-18. A draft for the user's approval; nothing is built yet. The user's notes are in `plans/notes-0607.md` (play-test of 0.60.5b). Code facts are from the 0.60.6 candidate, `starship-cadet.html` at `claude/cand-0606` (line numbers are from that file and will drift a little).*

## In short

The user's 17 notes fall into eight builds:

1. **Free time:** a new **leisure** need, **hobbies** you can do almost anywhere, and **tips** on how to spend an evening.
2. **Talking:** **Say hello** (the old Chat, with a smaller friendship bump), **Talk a while** (a meter like eating), **conversation options** inside it, crew who **stay put** while you talk, and a **Met / Acquaintances / Friends** roster.
3. **Traditions:** **2-3 a day**, overlaps allowed, **six new weekly traditions and one first-week one-off** so the first week is never thin, **Gym club** (renamed, with room for you), and a **7-day Calendar** that shows overlaps.
4. **Clothes:** change every morning at your **wardrobe**; after 5 days take the **hamper** to a **washer** and back. It first happens on **Day 2**, and Zeke warns you. **No laundry duty.**
5. **The Works crew at work:** some small repairs and deliveries go to the crew **in front of you**.
6. **The lounge bar:** a **bartender** and **a drink**.
7. **Washrooms:** **bathroom need half as fast**, a **toilet icon**, **no bench** in the Deck 3 washroom, **showers only on Deck 2 and Deck 4**.
8. **Tiles** in the **junior common room** at any time.

Six builders, a build master and one reviewer; about **4 to 5 hours** of wall-clock time from the go-ahead to a checked 0.60.7.

### N8. Talking: Say hello and Talk a while ("They shouldn't walk away when talking. Also an option to 'talk a while' to keep social going. It's a meter like when eating. It builds friendship. Current talk becomes say hello. Once a day too. But way less friendship bump than talking using convo options or the talk a while function.")

**Today.**
- Tapping someone: `tapCrew` (34155) sets `a.talking = 6` (6 real seconds), then `chatOpen` (29414). Buttons: **Chat** / Ask about... / Help? / Meet up, plus extras (`chatShow`, 29396).
- **Chat** is once a day (`r.chatDay`, 29403/29438): **+2 friendship** (`REL_PTS.chat`, 27470) and social +6 the first time (`CHAT_GAIN`, `playerChat`, 11274), times hygiene and bond multipliers.
- **Why they walk away:** the walker holds still only while `talking > 0` (`Walker.update`, 33833-33837); nothing refreshes it while the card is open, so after 6 seconds they walk back to their schedule, and `chatTick` (29503) closes the card when they're out of reach.
- Eating is a timed activity: `actStart` → `actUpdate` → `actEnd` with a progress bar (`actProgress` 11503, `actBar` 11510); the need fills by `gain / mins` per minute.

**Design.**
- **They stay put.** While the chat card is open and you're in reach, the person stays (refresh `talking` every frame), up to **60 real seconds idle** on the card. They only break off for something they can't miss: their shift starting within 10 ship minutes, sleep time, a tradition they host, an emergency (storm). Then they say a "got to go" line ("Shift in five. Catch me later?") and the card closes politely.
- **Say hello** replaces **Chat**: same button place, once a day per person. **+1 friendship** (was +2) and **+3 social** (was +6). Their line and a short reply from you. It's the quick nod in a corridor.
- **Talk a while** is a new button: a timed activity (`actStart('talk', ...)`), **15 ship minutes** (about 25 real seconds), **once a day per person**. You both stand facing (or both stay seated if they're sitting and there's a free seat beside them; you sit down). The person is held for the whole talk. The progress bar reads "Talking with Pell".
  - **It fills:** social +2 per ship minute (+30 for a full talk; with company bonuses like eating), leisure +5 at the end.
  - **Friendship: +1 for every 5 minutes, up to +3**, plus whatever the conversation option gives (N9, up to +2). So a good talk is **+5** against Say hello's **+1** (a meal together +3, working together +6, a favour +8).
  - **What you see:** every few seconds a line from them in the card (topics drawn from what they have: their hobby, home, their work, ship news, a tradition coming up, what they think of someone). One option set comes up partway through (N9).
  - **Stop early:** walk away or tap Stop and you keep what you earned; the day's talk is used.
  - **Who says no:** nobody refuses for being a stranger (it's a cozy game); they decline only for the same can't-miss reasons ("Love to, but I'm on in ten").
- **Ask about..., Help?, Meet up** stay as they are.
- **Arcs:** the story arcs (`arcBeat`) and moments are paced on friendship. With Say hello at +1 and Talk a while at up to +5, a player who talks a while with Tomas each day climbs **faster** than today; one who only says hello climbs slower. The social engineer re-runs `arc_check` with its diligent player using Talk a while, and checks every arc still reaches its last beat by Day 70.

**Who:** social engineer (section 20: the buttons, the talk activity's rules, gains); the held-walker change in section 10 is a few lines, written by the social engineer and reviewed by the sim engineer; the `talk` entry in `PLAYER_ACTS` is a hook the player engineer adds first. **Size:** M-L. **Risk:** medium (walker holds can leave someone stuck; the activity must end cleanly on deck change, shift, sleep, save). **Checks:** `player_check` (talk fills, ends, stop-early), `day_check` (a talked-to person is never late for a shift or stuck; nobody is held over 60 s idle), `arc_check`, a new `talk_check` step inside `player_check` (25 talks across the roster: no stuck walker, the once-a-day rule holds).
