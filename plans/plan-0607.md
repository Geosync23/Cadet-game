## In short

The user's 17 notes and 3 later ones fall into ten builds:

1. **Free time:** a new **leisure** need, **seven hobbies** (reading, writing and games on your device; drawing, knitting, puzzles, stargazing), and **tips** on how to spend an evening.
2. **Talking:** **Say hello** (the old Chat, with a smaller friendship bump), **Talk a while** (a meter like eating), **conversation options** inside it, crew who **stay put** while you talk, and a **Met / Acquaintances / Friends** tab in the Roster (the full roster stays the main view).
3. **Traditions:** **2-3 a weekday** (weekends keep all of theirs), overlaps allowed, **six new weekly traditions and one first-week one-off** so the first week is never thin, **Gym club** (renamed, with room for you), and a **7-day Calendar** that shows overlaps.
4. **Clothes:** change every morning at your **wardrobe**; after 5 days take the **hamper** to a **washer** and back. It first happens on **Day 2**, and Zeke warns you. **No laundry duty.**
5. **The Works crew at work:** some small repairs and deliveries go to the crew **in front of you**.
6. **The lounge bar:** a **bartender** and **a drink**.
7. **Washrooms:** **bathroom need half as fast**, a **toilet icon**, **no bench** in the Deck 3 washroom, **showers only on Deck 2 and Deck 4**.
8. **Tiles** in the **junior common room** at any time.
9. **Stores:** the right part from the **right shelf**, stock you can see, and a **Wednesday delivery** you unload with a hover cart (shelves unload themselves).
10. **The lift:** **two panels**, one on each side, and the panel **marks the deck your goal is on**.

Six builders, a build master and one reviewer; about **4 to 5 hours** of wall-clock time from the go-ahead to a checked 0.60.7.

**Design.**

You both stand facing (or both stay seated if they're sitting and there's a free seat beside them; you sit down). The person is held for the whole talk. The progress bar reads "Talking with Pell".

  - **Friendship: +1 for every 5 minutes, up to +3**, plus whatever the conversation option gives (N9, up to +2). So a good talk is **+5** against Say hello's **+1** (a meal together +3, working together +6, a favour +8).

  - **Who says no:** nobody refuses for being a stranger (it's a cozy game); they decline only for the same can't-miss reasons ("Love to, but I'm on in ten").

- **Arcs:** the story arcs (`arcBeat`) and moments are paced on friendship. **Who:** social engineer (section 20: the buttons, the talk activity's rules, gains); the held-walker change in section 10 is a few lines, written by the social engineer and reviewed by the sim engineer; the `talk` entry in `PLAYER_ACTS` is a hook the player engineer adds first. **Size:** M-L. **Risk:** medium (walker holds can leave someone stuck; the activity must end cleanly on deck change, shift, sleep, save). **Checks:** `player_check` (talk fills, ends, stop-early), `day_check` (a talked-to person is never late for a shift or stuck; nobody is held over 60 s idle), `arc_check`, a new `talk_check` step inside `player_check` (25 talks across the roster: no stuck walker, the once-a-day rule holds).
