A Veyli vessel needs help: the build spec

The coda after the career, Saturday to Tuesday, Days 27-30, as EP1's events plan (plans/events-plan-2.html, Part C) agreed it. A small Veyli ship has lost its coolant pump. The Wren keeps it company. You meet its engineer Taavek at the foot of the Tern's ramp, join the parts chain, make the part with Taavek, and watch the little ship light its engine from the bow glass. This page turns that into pieces, under your rules for these updates: tons of arrows, super obvious progression, and pronounced moments with dialogue.

From the events plan. Should helping count toward a commendation, like the storm did?

Recommended: yes, by the usual rule (two of its jobs, or one job and a meeting). It's real work, and your first in your new place. The other choice: no score, like the comet.

C2. See the Veyli ship outside the window?

From the events plan.

Recommended: yes, a small ship with three lights off the bow from Saturday afternoon to Tuesday morning, so the engine lighting up on Monday is something you watch. The other choice: words only, and Saruth reads the signal on the comm.

If you're not at the ramp by 09:35 on Sunday, someone has to meet Taavek.

Recommended: Zeke stands in, and you meet Taavek at the Works bay bench a few minutes later, where Taavek says the same things to you. The other choice: Taavek and Calder wait at the ramp until you come (up to an hour).
