Your first nine days: a checklist

For playtesting Pillar 5 Steps 1 and 2: from stepping off the shuttle (Day 0, a Sunday) to the second Monday (Day 8). Each day lists what should happen, in the order you meet it: who teaches you what, when and where, your jobs, the lessons, Mail and meetings. Tick each line when it happens. If one doesn't, note it in that day's box as "Day N, line M" (the number is under each line).

A normal shift day runs 09:00 to 17:00 (Fridays end at 15:30). The time clock opens at 08:00. Tea and biscuits are at 13:00 in the Works tea corner.
Times are ship times. "The arrow" is the gold marker that points where to go.
after the fixes only works once the three fix builds (arrows, the drill and scanner, the lessons) are in. On an older build, expect it to fail.
