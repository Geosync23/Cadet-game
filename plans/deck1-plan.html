Deck 1: Bridge

Sketch 2 (scaled up after the user's review of build 0.10.1; built as 0.12.x). Bow points forward, port side is on the left when facing it. The smallest deck, at the very top of the hull. Step out of the lift and the bridge doors are right ahead, with the observation room behind you, looking aft over the whole ship. Few people come up here, so every visit feels like an occasion. Tap any room. Rooms ringed in gold are new ideas, not in the design doc yet.
Deck 1 at a glanceThe bridge fills the bow: a viewscreen across the bow wall, a sunken command ring with a curved rail behind it, and the rear stations along the aft wall. The Captain's ready room is tucked aft of the bridge on the port side, beside the lobby, with its door on the bridge's aft edge; the washroom mirrors it to starboard. Aft of the lift, the observation room doubles as the conference room. The service layer is small: the relay room and a sub-floor crawl under the rear stations, plus a utility closet.22 × 12 tiles, still the smallest deck (Deck 6 is 36 × 12, Deck 2 below it 52 × 22). The bridge has about 85 standable tiles, up from 54 in Sketch 1. The lift lines up with every deck (game lift: [8, 6], car rows 4–6, ladder row 7, trunks row 3).
Open questions I had to guess at
  • Size. The design doc says about 14 × 10. Sketch 1 was 20 × 10 and the user found the bridge cramped, with the ready room crowding it. Sketch 2 is 22 × 12: the bridge is about 11 × 12 and has room to walk all the way round the command ring and the stations.
  • Ready room. Sketch 1 had it jutting into the bridge's port corner. It's now 3 × 4, aft of the bridge beside the lobby, and its door opens from the bridge's aft edge, clear of the ring.
  • Sunken ring. The build has no floor steps, so the ring is shown by a curved rail (a bar curve) behind the chairs. A real sunken floor would need a step kit and a floor-painter change.
  • Viewscreen. It sits on the bow hull as a dress piece that follows the curve, like the music room's stage. A wall curve in front of the hull would leave tiles behind it that nobody can reach. There are no bridge windows; the view is in the observation room.
  • Getting in. Bridge and observation room doors are drawn as open. Should the lift panel or the bridge doors stay shut (with tap text) until a story event calls you up?
  • Floors. The bridge has its own floor in the build: dark blue-grey carpet with the command ring inlaid.
  • Bridge washroom. This is a new idea. Keep it, or let the watch ride down to Deck 2?
  • Top of the spine. I've assumed the ladder and trunks end here, at a trunk head behind glass.

Utility rooms (proposed)

Deck 1's sealed service spaces get their insides, with nothing moved or resized: machines, a floor to stand and work on, and the hatch as the way in. Every machine has a machines entry, a panel tile beside it and a work:works place on that tile. Works crew and the player can pass the hatches; tier locks come later. Machine kinds are proposals for the systems engineer.
