  • The events plan (approved v4): the storm (Days 4-5) pauses Thursday's traditions after 14:00, and its all-clear (Friday 18:30) comes before Day 5's Sing-along. Day 8's creature leaves at 18:40, before Sato's talk about it. Day 14's Beach stories has the Veyli aboard. Day 18's Kit mend night ends before Echo Signal (20:30). Day 21's distress call (11:30-16:30) pauses that Sunday's afternoon traditions. Day 24's Loss of Power is a morning. Day 27's Glow paddle and Captain's lengths have no event near them.
