Wren talks, small and often

  • Twice a week: Mondays and Thursdays, 19:00-19:40 (after dinner opens, before Thursday's quiz night).
  • Small: 4 to 10 people. A ring of eight chairs on the auditorium stage, the house dark, the stars through the bow windows behind; anyone extra sits on the stage edge. Tea from the utility room's kettle.
  • Informal: one crew member shares something they know or love, then chat. No chair, no lectern, no Commander introducing anyone (v2's "Vance chairs" is gone).
  • Skips: the career's big days (1, 9-11, 15-16, 22-23, 26) and the storm (4-5), as v2 did. So the first four weeks have three talks, and from Day 29 it's twice every week.
