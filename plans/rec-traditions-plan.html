Rec dec traditions: twelve new ones for the Recreation Deck pitch for the user's approval · plan only

The user (2026-10-19): when the rec dec is switched on (after Pillar 5's Steps 3 and 4), it comes with about a dozen traditions of its own, "Sunday Lightdisc tournament, that kind of thing". Here are twelve: sport, quiet, social, creative and seasonal, fitted round the ship's existing traditions and the approved events plan. Each uses what the rec dec already has (the Lightdisc game, the tap-to-play pool, kayaks, wall and float room, the auditorium stage). Written by the Pillar 5 designer for lead 16. No game code.

In plain words

  • Twelve new traditions, all on the rec dec, on top of the five the rec dec plan already has (Winner Stays On on Sundays, the river regatta and Ring the bell every four weeks, Saturday film night and talent night in the auditorium).
  • A mix: four sporty (the Lightdisc Cup, Rookie court, Lanes night, Paddle club), three active-but-easy (Dawn swim, Wall night, Zero-G games), two quiet (Float hour, Glow paddle), two creative or social (Open stage, Wren talks) and one seasonal party (Season splash; Glow paddle is seasonal too).
  • Nothing new to learn: you join the way you join any tradition (be there, or tap a person or a place to join in); the sporty ones use the Lightdisc game and the pool, kayak, wall and float room animations the rec dec already plans. No times or scores except where Lightdisc already keeps them.
  • Small rewards: a little leisure and social, sometimes energy or hygiene, a friendship step with whoever hosts or plays beside you, a name on the foyer's boards. Never a penalty for not going.
  • No staff: the rec dec has none (the user's answer), so each tradition is hosted by a crew member who loves it.

How they fit (traditions, events, career)

  • What they move or replace: the Lightdisc Cup takes Winner Stays On's Sunday slot once every four weeks (Winner Stays On runs the other three). Nothing else moves; the junior common room stays the quiet Saturday hangout.
  • The events plan (approved v4): traditions pause in an event's hardest phases, as now. The storm (Days 4-5) pauses Thursday's Dawn swim only after 14:00, so nothing is lost. Day 8's Space Creature leaves at 18:40, before Rookie court and Sato's talk (which is about it). Day 13's Season splash happens with the Veyli aboard. Day 17's power surge is over by 15:00; Day 18's Echo Signal (20:30) is a Thursday, when the rec dec only has the dawn swim. Day 21's distress call (11:30-16:30) pauses Winner Stays On that afternoon (the regatta at 09:30 finishes first). Day 24's Loss of Power is a morning.
  • The career's big days: nothing on the rec dec is aimed at you on Days 9-11, 15-16, 22-23 or 26; Dawn swim before the Day 9 test and Wall night after it are there if you want them. On the night shift (Days 19-20) you sleep through Friday's Lanes night; it's a week off anyway.
  • Holidays (on hold): none clash if they return.
  • Buildable with what exists: the tradition engine (weekly, two-weekly, four-weekly and seasonal rhythms are all there), tap-to-join, the gatherings, the rec dec's planned animations and Lightdisc game, the talent night act picker and the meeting panel. New: the Cup's bracket (a small seeded draw), the boards' names, and a gentler Lightdisc ball for Rookie court.

Questions for the user (with my recommendations)

  1. Twelve, or fewer to start? Recommended: all twelve; the rec dec has a lot of rooms, and most are small.
  2. The Lightdisc Cup in place of Winner Stays On one Sunday in four? Recommended: yes, so Sunday afternoon is always Lightdisc and once a month it's the big one.
  3. Do you play the Cup's matches yourself (the live Lightdisc game), or are they settled for you? Recommended: you play them; the other pairs' results are seeded.
  4. Lanes night results seeded (never your speed)? Recommended: yes, like the regatta: it's about turning up for your department.
  5. Glow paddle once a season (four a year), or once a year? Recommended: once a season, so a long game sees it.
  6. Wren talks: can you give one later (as an Engine Specialist, on the drive)? Recommended: yes, a small moment after the career arc (a talk on the drive coils, with Torres in the front row).
