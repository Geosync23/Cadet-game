Deck 5: Operations

Sketch 3, updated to build 0.6.6. Bow points forward, port side is on the left when facing it. The working deck: you arrive by shuttle at the stern, step through the haulway to the lift, and the Works bay is right beside it, with a window onto the shuttle pad. Tap any room.
Deck 5 at a glanceThe Works complex: the bay with its job board and the Chief's glass office, a tea corner, a washroom, main storage and the machine shop. Aft, the shuttle bay with its door gear, and the cargo hold. Forward, the ship's machinery: the air plant, the fan hall and the manifold room, plus a sealed battery room, joined by pipe galleries along both hull sides.44 × 16 tiles, narrower than Deck 4. The lift lines up with Deck 4's. Built first (step 6c): the Works bay, tea corner, washroom, storage, machine shop, lobby, corridors and the shuttle bay floor. The fan hall can be watched through the window at the end of the corridor from day one.

Utility rooms (Pillar 2, proposed for review)

Deck 5's sealed rooms get their insides. All keep today's size and position. The cargo hold and the cart bay are fitted out here, and their stock and carts come with the supplies work (B3). 18 machines; the systems engineer reads them for wear. Each one gets a machines entry in DECKS[5] ({ id, kind, at, room, face, panel }), a panel tile where someone stands to service it, and a work:works place on that tile, facing the machine.

Air plant (a)

The air for every deck. The electrolyser on the stern wall makes oxygen from water and feeds the two banks of oxygen cells (glass columns, bubbling) on the port hull. The two main scrubber stacks stand in the bow corner. The Fan hall next door moves the air.

Way in: service hatch at h,38,5, from the Fan hall; also a hatch from the port pipe gallery (v,34,0).
