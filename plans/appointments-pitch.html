Appointments worth keeping

You said appointments need to be more interesting. This page looks at what an appointment is today, then pitches ten new kinds, each with something that actually happens when you turn up. Nothing is built. Answer each one at the bottom.

What an appointment is today

The Appointments app lets you pick a person, a time and a room (the mess, a lounge, the gym, the garden, the common room, the tea corner, the music room, the observatory, the infirmary, the Chief's office, the promenade). Crew answer from their real day, and accepted ones go in their plans. The game also makes a few itself:

So the special ones (trainings, the Captain, the Chief) work well, but the everyday ones, the ones you make yourself and the cabin visits, have nothing in them. The pitch below gives every appointment a reason: something you do together, something you see, and usually something you keep.

First, the cheap fix for all of them

Any appointment you make with someone plays one of their own "Talk a while" talks when you sit down (the main characters now have thirty each). So even a plain "meet me at the tea corner" has a real conversation in it. It reuses what's built, so it's small to build. It's recommended whatever you pick below.

RecommendedGalley · BrennanSmall to medium

A page in Brennan's book

Chef Brennan keeps a book of everyone's favourite dish. One afternoon after the lunch rush she asks you into the galley to taste three things and tell her yours. Your pick goes in the book, and from then on it turns up on a Wednesday menu now and then (Wednesday is already "a crew member's favourite" day).

Who, when
Brennan invites you once you know her a little (acquaintance), a weekday at 14:30.
You do
Taste three spoonfuls, pick one. Three choices, none wrong.
You keep
Your dish on the menu board, your name under it, and crew who mention it.
She says
"Sit. Spoon. Try this one first. No, don't be polite, love. Tell me which."

The night round

Kofi Mensah walks the whole ship once a night and says good night to every deck by name: Chief Holt walks every deck in the mornings, Mensah does the nights. When you're friends he asks if you'd like to come. You walk it with him, deck by deck, and he tells you one thing about each that only the night shift knows.

Who, when
Mensah invites you at friend, a night you're off the next morning, 22:30.
You do
Walk with him, lift to lift (it uses the walking gathering the Lap of the Wren has), about 40 minutes.
You keep
A Record line, and later he says good night to your bunk room by name.
He says
"Good night, Deck 4. Short and sweet. She likes it short."

An hour in another department

Ask someone from another department if you can follow them for an hour: the galley at prep, the infirmary's morning clinic, Survey's lab, the garden at first watering, the Watch office. You watch, they explain, and once you get to try one small thing (a seedling, a label, a stir of the pot).

Who, when
Anyone you're a colleague with, during their shift, once a week.
You do
A short meeting in their room: a few lines, one small "you try" choice.
You keep
"Spent an hour with Survey" in your Record, and a little standing with that department.
Note
Never the bridge: the bridge stays special (your rule). The Watch office and the observatory are fine.

Teach me your thing

Ask someone to teach you their hobby. Zeke teaches you to knit (already promised in the game: "Zeke can teach you to knit"); Cruz shows you how to take a cutting; Raman lets you help with one part of his model Wren; Rina teaches you Tiles, badly on purpose; Kovac shows you how she strips a scrubber in nine and a half minutes.

Who, when
Anyone whose roster hobby it is, at colleague, off duty.
You do
Half an hour of the hobby side by side; it counts double toward your hobby.
You keep
The hobby unlocked if you didn't have it, and the supplies the first time.
Zeke says
"Needle here. Wool round. No, round. There. That's a stitch. Look at you."

Book a late snack in your bunk room with your bunkmates (a group appointment, already possible). Someone brings the grab shelf's best, someone tells a story, and someone falls asleep first.

Who, when
You, with two or more bunkmates, after 22:00 on a night before a day off.

The bridge · RamanMedium

The spare helm chair

Once you're invited to the bridge and close friends with Dev Raman, he asks you up for a quiet night watch. You sit in the spare chair while he flies a slow, gentle turn, and he tells you how it feels.

Who, when
Raman, close friend, after your bridge invite and after his own story has ended, once. It never retells his story.
You do
Sit and watch the stars swing round the windows.
You keep
A Record line: "Sat at the helm with Dev Raman."
Note
The bridge stays special: by invitation only, once, near the end of a friendship. Not something you can book.

My recommendation

Start with the cheap fix plus four: A page in Brennan's book, The night round, An hour in another department and Teach me your thing. Two are invitations and two are things you ask for. They cover four corners of the ship, and each leaves something visible behind: a dish on the menu, a goodnight, a Record line, a hobby. The rest can follow.
