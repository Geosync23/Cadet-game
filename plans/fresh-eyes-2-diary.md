# Fresh-eyes diary 2: Days 0 to 8 as a new player (build 0.92.0)

FE2, the second fresh-eyes player. Build: claude/integration 685af94 (0.92.0), game file sha256 f2329b62...6ebd (checked). Teaching arrows on (Settings default "First times"), the controller and Crossing the Line off. Phone size 390 x 844, touch, Chromium. I play only from what is on the screen: no code, no design docs, no dev shortcuts.

**How bad:** *blocks me* (I could not go on, or lost something I was meant to do), *confusing* (I worked it out or guessed, but a new player might not), *small* (looks or wording). *Good* marks what worked.

**Speed.** On this test machine the game draws about 3 frames a second, so ship time runs about 6 times slower than on a phone (about 10 real seconds per ship minute). I played in real time and only waited (no skipping) through quiet hours.

**Screenshots:** `plans/fresh-eyes-2/` (the name of each picture is given in brackets).

Item numbers match the batches sent to the lead.

## Day 0, Sunday (arrival, 17:15)

The manifest card ("Your name", "Sign and go aboard") is clear. The card behind it still says "You wake in Bunk 1" while I'm on the shuttle (FE1 item 1, still there). *Small.*

**4. 17:15-17:35, shuttle bay.** Zeke talks in place for about 16 ship minutes (lovely lines: "I talk to the coils. Out loud."; "Ask me anything, by the way. Tap me."), then walks off. Nothing says "follow him". *Small.*

**5. 17:34-17:41, shuttle bay.** Single taps on the floor beyond the shuttle-bay rail did not move me; press-and-hold did. *Confusing.*

**1. 17:51-18:28, Main lift, Deck 5: the tour stalls. Blocks me.** Zeke walks me to the lift ("This is where we'll be most days. You'll love it. I do."), then stands by the spine ladder with a gold marker over him, silent, for 35+ ship minutes. There is no arrow, no "Four, please" and no tag. Tracked shows only "Dinner: Now" (Deck 3) and tomorrow's rows. (d0-09, d0-17)

**2. Same place. Zeke can't be tapped. Confusing.** Five taps on his body or name label open no card, though floor taps walk me fine. On the shuttle he had just said "Tap me".

**3. 18:08, lift.** With no instruction I tapped the keypad beside the lift door: the deck list opened first time (*good*: better than FE1's run). I chose Deck 3 because Dinner said "Now". Only on arrival at Deck 3 did an arrow appear: "Tap the lift: Deck 5 · Zeke Torres". The lift list marked Deck 5 "-> Tracked, Your arrow's deck", which is clear and nice (d0-15). Back on Deck 5, Zeke still stood silent. So the arrow pulled me back to a Zeke who then did nothing. *Confusing.*

**6. 18:38-19:22, Deck 4.** I took Zeke down to Deck 4 myself (he came along). He stood silent again, then walked off along the junior corridor with only an edge ring "Zeke Torres". At 19:22 the tour picked up: "Deck 4. Quarters. The junior wing's this way." In all the tour stalled for about 90 ship minutes. *Confusing.*
- While following him, a tap meant for the floor opened Marta Soto's card through a wall (d0-30).

**7. 19:26-19:32, Bunk 1. Good.** "Put your bag in your wardrobe": I did it myself this time ("You put your bag and your things away... Your hands are free."). The device hand-over opened straight on Mail, and Zeke's "Before you land" letter is lovely ("By Thursday it's just home with a lift in it.").

**8. 19:45, Mail from Calder: "Missed you at dinner." Blocks me (as for FE1).** It came while the tour was still taking me to dinner, and while Tracked and the device both said "Dinner: Now, the mess". Dinner runs until 20:30, so the Mail is early as well as unfair.

**9. 19:33-20:02, Deck 4 lift lobby. Confusing.** The arrow said "Tap the lift: Deck 3 · Zeke Torres". Press-and-hold toward the edge ring walked me into Bunk 5, then Bunk 7 (d0-42, d0-46). A floor tap by the bench sat me on the bench. I could see the keypad, but taps from the lobby did not open it. At 20:02 the arrow changed to "Head to your bunk when you're done looking around", and the lift list and the device opened on top of each other (d0-60).

**10. 20:12-20:23, mess hall. Confusing.** Tracked's Dinner arrow cleared on arrival, with no pointer to the servery. Two floor taps sat me in chairs ("Seat. You sit down."). I found the Drinks station, then the Servery.

**11. 20:23-20:45, eating. Good (FE1 item 17 fixed).** Queue, then "Your turn. You take a tray: tacos." I tapped a chair: "You sit down to tacos. About 17 minutes", "Eating · 15 min" under Tracked, "Food 100" (d0-76).

**12. 21:00-21:11, Deck 3 lift. Blocks me for 11 minutes.** People covered the keypad: 6 taps opened 4 people's cards (Hamid Larsen 3 times, Theo Teague, Petra Jovanovic twice, Ravi Hart). Then a tap near the ladder opened the lift list. Its "Your arrow's deck" tag sits on top of "You are here" on the Commons row (d0-96).

**13. 21:24-21:52, Bunk 1: getting into bed. Confusing.** The camera flips between two angles on almost every tap in Bunk 1, so the pinned bunk jumps about. Nine taps on the pin all gave the same card: "Bunk: Lukas's bunk, and the empty berth above it: the only spare bed on the ship. Yours is the next one." I read that as "the pin is on someone else's bed" (d0-113). At 21:52 "You climb into your bunk" and the Alarm card appeared, about 30 s after my last tap (d0-120). It looks as if the tap walks me slowly round the bunk while the "Lukas's bunk" card stays up, so I couldn't tell anything was happening.

**14. Small.** Captain Marrow's and Lucia Ferreira's cards are warm. Sleep from 21:52 to 06:00 is instant. "You chat for a minute." is added to the same bottom line again and again ("...Food 100. You chat for a minute. You chat for a minute.").

**Day 0 summary.** The tour is warm but stalls twice for long stretches, nothing tells you to follow Zeke, and you still get "Missed you at dinner" while you're on your way there. Eating works now. The lift keypad is findable, but people stand on it.

## Day 1, Monday (first work day)

06:00, "Good morning. Your alarm's going." Food 79, Energy 100, Hygiene 40, Bathroom 20. Tomas Lind's "Morning! Washroom first, before the queue." is a nice nudge.

**15. 06:05-06:40, Port washroom. Good.** Settling in "Washed and fed: Go there" put an arrow on the washroom. Tapping the pin queued me for the shower (10 min, Hygiene 100), then the toilet, then "Now wash your hands at the basins." Settling in moved to 1 of 17. *Small:* Tomas Lind's "Ask about..." offers News / Their work / Home, nothing about the washroom the list told me to ask him about.

**16. 06:40, Tracked. Good (FE1 complaint fixed).** The followed row now shows "TRACKING" (d1-16). *Small:* after the washroom the arrow switched itself to "On shift" without my asking.

**17. All day, Tracked list. Confusing.** The list's "Close" button sits over the last rows. When the list is long, Close goes off the bottom of the screen and I have to scroll for it. Twice my "Close" tap followed a row instead (Sunrise stretch, then The call booths). The open list also covers Rotate (d1-87).

**18. 06:48, Bunk 1. Confusing.** I wanted to change out of yesterday's clothes. There are several wardrobes, none labelled or pinned as mine, and my taps gave "Lukas's bunk..." (d1-23). I gave up.

**19. 07:10-07:28, mess. Good.** "Grab and go" at the counter: "You take a hot plate of porridge and eggs. About 2 minutes", Food 99 (d1-51).

**20. Note (my test browser, not the game).** The browser hit its 2-hour limit at 07:42 and the game reloaded "Welcome back. Day 1, Monday 07:06" (saved on reaching the deck). I lost 36 minutes, breakfast included. A second restart at 14:51 reloaded the 14:00 save. Saving on deck arrival is a real improvement on hourly saves.

**21. 07:06-07:28 (after the reload), mess. Confusing.** I couldn't find the servery again: 6 Rotates, and 4 floor taps near tables sat me down. Tracking Breakfast inside the mess shows no pointer (FE1 34). I went to work at Food 73.

**22. All day, cards. Small but constant.** A card's × hit box sits about 20 px below and right of the drawn ×. My first tap on the drawn × often misses, and a second tap falls through to the floor and walks me off somewhere (about 10 times today). The × also moves up and down as the card text changes.

**23. 07:40-08:50, Deck 5. Good.** FE1 36 is fixed: the arrow carried on after the lift ("Works bay, Deck 5"). The warnings "Morning briefing starts in 15 min · Go there" (08:35), "Your shift starts in 15 min" (08:45) and "...is starting" (08:50) are clear. Tips at 07:56, 08:00 ("Clock in by the tea corner at shift start") and 08:30 are helpful.

**24. 08:50-09:09, Monday briefing. Confusing.** The speakers are still "tech1" (line 4) and "tech2" (lines 5 and 11) (FE1 37), and the count goes 16/17 then 17/18 (d1-94). The briefing ends at 09:09 waiting on my answer ("Ready when you are / What if I get something wrong? / (Nod)"), after the shift has started. So sitting through the whole briefing makes you late.

**25. 09:11-09:35, time clock. Blocks me (late again).** "YOUR JOB: Now, Clock in" gives an arrow, and the pin is on the clock. Tapping it gives "Time clock: Not clocked in. Your shift began at 09:00: the time clock's past the tool wall", which tells me where the clock is while I'm standing at it (FE1 38, unchanged) (d1-103). Zeke stood on the spot; taps hit the tool wall and Zeke's card. The clock opened at 09:35: "Clocked in at 09:35. A little late. It happens." (d1-111).

**26. 09:42, job board. Good.** It opened first tap, with the pin on the board itself (FE1 46 fixed): "Repair: Light relays 1, asked for you", "Take it", then "Job taken... parts first" and the arrow "Fetch a relay: the Lights shelf" (d1-114).

**27. 09:45-11:53, main storage: the relay. Blocks me (FE1 40 and its top-10 item 2).** I could see the glowing Lights shelf, but my taps went to Marta Soto's card 9 times, Signe Moreau's 5, Emeka Halloran's once, the Check-out counter 4 times ("Relays are on the Lights shelf: it's lit up for you") and the Filters shelf 12 or more times ("No relays on the Filters shelf..."). Marta and Signe stood in front of the shelf the whole two hours. Two × misses walked me off to the Works washroom and the fan hall. The camera swings after each step, so I needed Rotate after nearly every tap (d1-131, d1-147, d1-164). "Help?" on Signe's card is me offering help ("Kind of you. Nothing right now."), as FE1 found.

**28. 12:52-12:55, the glowing shelf answers as "Filters". Probably a bug.** Once Marta and Signe stepped aside, I stood right against the glowing shelf (d1-186, d1-187). Tapping the middle of the glow gives "Filters: No relays on the Filters shelf. Relays are on the Lights shelf: it's lit up for you." Either the glow is on the wrong shelf, or the Lights shelf's tap area is hidden behind Filters. I never got a relay, so my first job never got going.

**29. 12:45-13:30, tea. Confusing.** "Tea and biscuits starts in 15 min · Go there" is good. Getting there took 40 minutes: taps walked me round the Works bay, the lift lobby, the Works washroom and the main corridor, past "After you." and "Excuse me" blocks. I arrived at 13:30. The tea arrow cleared on arrival with no seat shown, and one tap later I was back out in the bay. Tea missed.

**30. 16:54-17:22, clocking out. Blocks me for 25 minutes (FE1 55).** "Clock out at the time clock" came in good time. Then the whole crew gathered round the clock for Name on the beam, and my taps opened Pell Okafor's card 3 times and Bea Silva's once, or walked me into the lift lobby, the main corridor and the tea corner. The 17:06 tip "Tap the beam to paint your name. The Works crew are waiting to see it go up." came while I was still clocked in (d1-212). The clock finally opened at 17:21: "On shift since 09:35. Today: 7.8 h." Clocked out at 17:22: "Evening's yours."

**31. 17:22-17:31, Name on the beam. Good: the best moment so far.** The arrow "Your name goes on the beam", then "Zeke hands you the pen. You paint your first name on the beam, beside everyone who's served in Works." Then "Your name is on the beam. The whole bay cheers. Welcome to Works." Davi: "Lovely letters. That's there for good." Zeke: "Welcome to Works, cadet. Properly, now." (d1-226, d1-227-beam). The tip at 17:32 ("Off shift. Your evening is yours: call home..., go to a ship tradition (Calendar), talk a while with someone, or try a hobby.") is just right.
- *Small:* Calder's Mail "Thanks for coming" says "Good to see you at name on the beam. Same time next one?" ("name" in lower case, and "next one" sounds odd for a once-only welcome). Nothing is said about my unfinished relay job.

**32. 17:28, "Starving: tasks are 15% slower."** It came with no dinner yet, after a day in which I couldn't get to food.

**33. 18:02, Tracked. Small.** My relay job now shows as "Your job / Your job" (its description is gone), and "Name on the beam: Now" is still listed after I've done it (d1-236).

**34. 18:08, mess: a new teaching arrow. Good, but then blocked.** On entering the mess an arrow said "Get your dinner at the servery" (d1-239), which fixes item 10 and FE1 34. But at dinner time the mess is packed. From 18:08 to 18:46 I couldn't reach the servery: holds barely moved me, taps opened Ilse Quist, Chef Brennan, Theo Teague, Ivo Madsen and Rina Deshi (twice), and the camera swung every step. *Blocks me.*

**35. 18:56-19:20, my meal stopped by itself. Probably a bug.** I got there by tapping the floor in front of the counter (taps on the counter itself did nothing): queue, then a tray of "Brennan's spiced lentils", and a good new tip: "Take your tray to any free seat and tap it to eat. Company at the table lifts your spirits too." I sat on a long-table bench: "You sit down to Brennan's spiced lentils. About 24 minutes." I waited without touching anything. By 19:20 the message had become "Long table: Scratched initials on the bench... You sit down. To eat, queue at the servery for a tray, then bring it to a seat." The Eating line was gone, the tray was gone and Food had gone from 5 to 2 (d1-281, d1-282). A "Hobby" button had appeared.

**36. 19:36-19:52, second tray, no seat. Blocks me.** I queued again (19:36) and got a tray. Then 7 taps on empty chairs in 15 minutes opened Thea Grieve (twice), Wen Takahashi, Bo Mbeki (twice) and Leila Eklund's cards, or moved me a step so the camera swung and the chairs moved (d1-300). I gave up with the tray in my hands and Food at 2.

**37. Small.** Zeke's card says "Engine officer · stranger" after a whole day together, and Pell, Signe and Marta are still "stranger" after we've talked.
