More traditions to choose from

You said "good ideas but need more ideas for traditions". Here are 22 new ones, of every kind: food, games, music, crafts, garden and rec deck, quiet ones, competitive ones, silly ones, skills, ship work, home customs and one seasonal. They're spread across the week, from 08:30 to 23:30, on five decks and the rec deck, with 22 different hosts. Tick the ones you want at the bottom.

Since your answers to round 1

  • Scone morning: yes. It's in (Sundays 09:30, every 6 weeks from week 10, Halloran).
  • The other eight (comics wall day, hum night, Sato's tea, map night, paper boat race, newsletter night, learn a dance, a thousand cranes): no. They're parked, not deleted, and none of them is reused below.
  • Six take turns: yes. The slots they free are in the next section, with a new one suggested for each.
  • Week 1: stays as it is. Every new one starts in week 2 or later.
  • The new hand's tea: "this one can go". We've read that as retiring the Thursday welcome tea for new crew (parked). One knock-on: Basil's crew story has its big scene at that tea on Day 25, so it would need a new place (for example, his first Friday tea in Works). Tell us if you meant something else.
  • The event swap: a short visit, Days 16-19; the comet on Friday Day 26 at 21:30; your night shifts stay on Days 19-20; Commander Vance's memo and the senior briefing move into the visit's week. These go to the builders as decided.

Five-minute skills

Skill-sharing

Each time Bilal brings along a different crewmate to teach one small thing in five minutes: tie a bowline, fold a fitted sheet, splice a cable, fix a squeaky headphone. Then everyone tries.

Place
Bow lounge, Deck 3
Host
Bilal Nakamura (Watch; patient, proud)
Fits
Between shift end and gym club (19:15). Nothing else is in the bow lounge on a Monday evening.
SAT 15:00-16:00 · every 2 weeks from week 2 (even weeks)

The coffee machine trials

Silly, competitive

Three people aboard each say they are the best at fixing the coffee machine. Every other Saturday each makes a cup, and the room tastes them blind and votes. Calder reminds everyone the machine is Works's job.

Place
Bow lounge, Deck 3 (the bar)
Host
Maeve Volkov, with Elif Yoon and Dara Shah
Fits
Fills the Tiles ladder's freed hour on even weeks.

Valo post day

Home custom

On Valo, letters go out with a sprig of something green folded inside. Vera and Tariq, both from Valo, show everyone how: write a page home on paper, fold it the Valo way, tuck in a leaf from the garden, into the bag.

Place
Comms room, Deck 2 (where the post bag waits)
Host
Vera Ueda, with Tariq Varga
Fits
Takes the postcard night hour on the weeks postcards are off, in a different room.
