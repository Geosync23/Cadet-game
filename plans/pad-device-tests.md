# Controller: things to try on your real phone and controller

Written by SD2 for lead 43, 2026-10-28 (Wave C of plans/controller-audit.md). The test browser can't prove what a real phone allows, so these are for you to try with your own controller before the controller is switched on in the game.

**How to try them:** open the game in your phone's browser (not inside the Claude app) with the controller link the lead sends you. Connect your controller first and don't touch the screen. Each test says what to do, and what you should see. Write OK or what happened next to each.

## Sound

1. **Sound from the controller alone.** Open the game fresh and press any controller button before you touch the screen.
   - Good: the ship hum starts. Or, if your browser doesn't allow that, a short note says "Sound starts at your first tap", once.
   - Then tap the screen once: the hum should start straight away.
2. **The note only once.** After the note, put the controller down, tap the screen once, then pick the controller up again.
   - Good: the note doesn't come back.
3. **Sound Off stays off.** Turn Sound off in Settings, reload, and press a controller button.
   - Good: no sound and no note.

## Saves (the Saves app)

4. **Download my save, by controller.** Press Y to open your device, move to Saves, press A, move to Download my save and press A.
   - Good: your phone saves a file (or asks where to save it).
   - If nothing happens, tap Download my save with your finger instead. The app shows a line saying this may need a tap.
5. **Copy save as text.** This button only shows if a download couldn't happen. Try it by controller, then paste into a note.
   - Good: the save's text pastes.
6. **Load a save file, by controller.** In Saves, move to Load a save file and press A.
   - Good: your phone's file picker opens.
   - If it doesn't, tap the button with your finger (the hint line covers this). Then pick the file you downloaded in test 4 and choose a slot.
7. **Delete's guard.** Move to Delete on a slot you don't need and press A.
   - Good: it asks "Delete Slot N?". Choose Keep it and nothing is lost.

## Words on screen

8. **"Tap" words become buttons.** On a new game, the "Tap your device" pointer should read "Press Y for your device" while you use the controller. The laundry and hamper tips should name the button, not "tap".
   - Touch the screen and the words go back to "tap".

## The phone itself

9. **The Back button or gesture with a clip-on controller.** Press the controller's B several times on the deck with no panel open, and use the phone's own Back gesture once.
   - Good: the game doesn't leave the page or reload.
10. **Battery or Bluetooth drop.** Turn the controller off while walking.
    - Good: you stop at once and the game pauses ("Controller disconnected"). Turn it back on and press Start or A to carry on.
