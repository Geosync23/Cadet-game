Crossing the Line: the plan

Event planner EP2, 2026-10-21, version 1.1 (for review: the story added at your request). Branch claude/event-plan-line, from build 0.64.6. Planning only: the game hasn't changed. The first ship-wide event in the story, built after the ion storm. Your answers go in section 8.
In short
  • Sunday Day 7, your second day off, so Saturday stays completely free. The Wren crosses into the Veyli reach. One day, no score, nothing breaks.
  • 14:30: the ship's horn on every deck, the hull lights turn gold until the supper ends, and the Captain's short address. She's off that day, is called in, and jokes about it.
  • 14:30-15:15, the plaza: about 50-60 crew. You stand at the front with the other first-timers. The Captain reads the old oath, you choose one reply, then everyone drinks a mug of Brennan's famously awful green Line punch.
  • 18:00-19:15, the mess: the Line supper takes the Sunday table's place, up to about 60 people.
  • Monday: your Line certificate on your shelf, a brass plaque on the plaza, and the crew retelling who pulled the worst face.
  • A small story runs through it, as you asked: recommended, "Who answers the oath", with Tomas, your bunkmate, who came aboard the same week as you. What you choose on the plaza is cast on Monday's plaque.
  • About 5-6 build turns, switched off until you've reviewed it. 9 questions for you, each with a recommended answer (section 8).

The story (new)

Each option has a beginning, a middle where your one choice matters, and a payoff on Monday. All three use crew already aboard, and none touches the later chapters of anyone's personal story.

A. "Who answers the oath" (recommended)
  • Saturday night. The old hands' wind-up for every crossing: "The newest hand answers the oath, in front of everyone. It's always a disaster." (Pell starts it.) Tomas, your bunkmate, arrived the same week as you and believes every word. After his shift he can't settle in Bunk 1: "Eighty people. I'll say something stupid. Or nothing." You can practise with him, tell him he'll be fine, or admit you're nervous too. If you're not there by 22:30, he sends you a Mail instead: "Can't sleep. Tomorrow. You?"
  • Sunday, 14:40, the plaza. The wind-up is half true. After the oath the Captain asks, "Who answers for the first-timers?" Tomas is in the front row in his Watch uniform: he's on shift, keeping the plaza, and she's pulled him forward ("Watch can spare you for ten minutes"). Your one choice:
    • "I'll answer." You: "We came a long way. We'd like to stay."
    • Nudge Tomas. He wobbles, then: "We're new, and we're nervous, and we came anyway."
    • "We'll answer together." Both: "We'll look after each other. That's the whole oath, isn't it?"
    Then Brennan's punch.
  • Monday. The brass plaque carries the words you chose, under the date. The crew quote them back. Tomas has a line for each ending (proud of you; amazed at himself, "I said it. Out loud. In front of the Captain."; or glad he wasn't alone).
  • If you miss it, Tomas answers alone with the plain line ("We're new, and we're nervous, and we came anyway."), it goes on the plaque, and he tells you all about it on Monday.
  • No spoilers (checked with the writer): his nerves are only about speaking in front of everyone; "We'd like to stay" is only ever your answer, and Tomas never picks it up; nothing from the later chapters of his story.
  • Who lets him go: a Watch hand on shift ("Go on, Lind. I've got the plaza."). Chief Holt is off that Sunday, and the writer keeps Holt's own moment with Tomas for later in Tomas's story.
  • Cost: about one more turn (the Saturday moment, the reworked oath, the plaque's words, Tomas's Monday lines; about 30 new lines from the writer).
B. "The missing recipe"
  • Sunday morning: Brennan's Line punch recipe card is gone. Lukas tidied the galley, and a box of old cards went to the swap meet.
  • The swap meet (10:30-12:30): you can find the card on a stall, then give it back, suggest a new recipe (the punch comes out a different green), or keep the secret and let Brennan improvise.
  • Monday: the card is framed beside the plaque, or the new recipe becomes "the Wren's own".
  • Weaker: it all happens before the ceremony, and the oath isn't part of it.
C. "The Line roll"
  • Saturday: Commander Vance keeps the Wren's Line roll, signed by every first-timer since the ship launched, and asks you to bring it up from stores.
  • The ceremony: each first-timer signs it, and you choose what to write beside your name.
  • Monday: the roll hangs by the plaque, with your line at the bottom.
  • Weaker: quiet, and nobody else's story is in it.

Reactions come within minutes of the horn, so the lines the crew say from 14:30 are about the horn, the gold windows and the walk to the plaza ("Did you feel that? The whole deck went gold."). Lines about the punch and the answer wait until the ceremony is over at 15:15.
