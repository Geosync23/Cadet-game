  • Engine work (night 1) and the reactor (night 2). Service a coil, swap a control board, balance two coils against each other; coolant readings become repairs. The reactor is reserved for Engine Specialists: a permit signed on the bridge by the night watch, a dosimeter badge from the rack, a short careful visit, the Watch told when you go in and out. Nothing frightening and no fail.
  • Leading a team (night 3). A big job (a coil swap, the recycler's membrane bank, the main fan's bearing) done while the drive runs slow at night: you pick two or three of the night crew (friends say yes faster), book the slot with the bridge's night watch, give each a spot and call the steps.   • Off nights (Day 25, 06:00). Calder comes down at the end of night 3: Navarro is done upstairs, and you have a cadet tomorrow. "Go to bed." The rest of Thursday is yours; Basil's new hand's tea is that evening.
  • Mentoring (Day 26). Basil Bramwell, the cadet who came off the Tern on Sunday, is with you for the day. He watches you do a job, then you watch him; he gets stuck on a head-scratcher and you pick which clue to point him to; he's late once and you choose what to say.
