Head-scratcher screens (mock-ups)

Three sketches of the Fault notes page on a phone, for Pillar 5's added deductive repair puzzles (plans/pillar5-plan.md, Part 6). The device slides up over two thirds of the screen in its cyan old-screen style; the ship stays visible and keeps running above it. Everything is tap only, with targets of at least 44 px.
