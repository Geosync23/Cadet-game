Rec dec mini-games: the wall, the pool, the kayaks plan v1 · plan only

The user (2026-10-19): "each sport (other than floating) should be a little mini game, that's the goal. Design a game / digital toy for the rock wall, the pool and the kayak." Lightdisc already is one. This page offers two ideas for each of the three with my pick, all built the way Lightdisc is. Written by the Pillar 5 designer for lead 16. No game code.

What all three share (the Lightdisc way)

  • One thumb, short goes: 30 seconds to 3 minutes a go. You only steer or time; the rest is automatic.
  • The live 3D room: tap the wall, a lane or the kayak dock, walk there, and the camera eases into the game view over about 0.8 s (Lightdisc's dolly-zoom). The near walls fade as the cutaway already does. At the end a result card (Again, Done) and the camera eases back.
  • The clock stops for the go and its result, as Lightdisc and Tiles do (a PAUSE flag each).
  • No failing: nobody falls badly, sinks or capsizes for good. A fall ends the go gently and keeps what you managed.
  • Crew alongside: crew who are free can join (Lightdisc's way of finding someone). They're played by the same rules with a seeded autopilot, and off-screen their result is worked out on paper, so traditions (the regatta) need no drawing.
  • Rewards: leisure and fitness (as Lightdisc: fitness up, energy a little down), hygiene for the pool, and a friendship step with whoever went with you. Personal bests are kept and said in words ("your best climb yet").

Climbing wall

FallingGrip runs out (or three misses): the auto-belay lowers you slowly, and the height you reached counts.

CrewThe wall has two auto-belays: a friend climbs the other route beside you (Tomas on Wall night). "Race me?" turns it into a race to the bell.

Why Reach: it feels like climbing (choosing your next hold), and the four coloured routes give it somewhere to go over weeks. Bell rush is quicker to build but a plain timing game.

Pool

★ A. LengthsB. Ring dive
The toySwim four lengths in rhythm. A ring closes in on your swimmer; tap anywhere as it meets the beat for a clean stroke (good timing = smooth speed, mistimed = a splash and a slower stroke). Every few strokes, hold your thumb down to breathe. At the wall, swipe up for a tumble turn.Dive from the side and steer underwater with your thumb (where your thumb is, you go) to pick up rings on the Wren mosaic before your breath runs out; come up, breathe, dive again.
What you seeThe live pool, the camera above and behind your lane, looking down it towards the hull windows and the stars; the lane ropes either side.Above the water from the side, then under it: the mosaic and caustic light (a new underwater look).
A goabout 45 to 70 seconds (four short lengths)1 to 2 minutes (three dives)

CrewFour lanes, one swimmer each: up to three crew swim beside you. "Race me?" makes it a race; otherwise they keep their own pace.

Why Lengths: it's what a pool is for, it fits Dawn swim exactly, and it stays above the water (no new underwater look to build). Ring dive was dropped as a tradition (you said neither), but it could still come later as a toy.

Building it (notes for the rec deck's owner)

  • Reuse Lightdisc's kit (section 23b, plans/handover-lightdisc.md): pure rules at a fixed 60 Hz with no R() or Math.random; a clone for checking that a live go matches the go on paper at 60, 30 and 20 fps; the view switch (discViewOn/discFit-style camera, the #ld overlay, body.ld-on hiding the HUD); the ask-and-find flow (discFind, crewComeTo); FullPerson partners with their crowd body hidden; the save pattern (SHIP.disc read defensively). One mini-game module per sport, each with New, Step, Paper and Clone.
  • Seeds: Lightdisc uses schedU keys 830-832; the three games need about two each. Proposed 833-838, to be checked against the key list first.
  • Phones: under 200 draw calls in a go (Lightdisc is about 129). Wall holds and gate poles as one merged mesh or an InstancedMesh each; no new lights; the one water material (RK.poolWater); nothing on the water's plane (gates and lane ropes sit above it, the stroke ring is screen overlay).
  • Poses: new rig poses like discPose: reach and pull, the stroke and the turn, the paddle stroke. You are drawn by the activity's stand-in body, as in Lightdisc.
  • Checks: a check per game like disc_check.py (rules, save, the frozen clock, draw calls, the frame-rate match), and Decks 1-6 identical in cadet_test.py.
  • Changes the rec deck plan: its line "Pool, kayaks, wall and float room: a tap plays the animation. No practice, no times" becomes "a tap plays the mini-game; the float room stays a tap".
