The crew of the CSV Wren

Everyone aboard, for Pillar 1 of the Living Ship. Grouped by department, most senior first. Each card shows when they work, their days off, where they sleep and where they work. Names marked Provisional name are first ideas and can still change. Story crew are the hand-written characters. The coloured dot is their uniform: red for command, gold for operations, blue-green for sciences.

75 crew, you included, plus Envoy Saruth as a guest. 1 Captain, 1 Executive Officer, 4 Department Chiefs, 6 Senior Officers, 16 Officers and 47 Junior Officers. 60 work days and 15 work nights. Everyone has two days off in a row, staggered round the week so every department always has people on. Beds: 12 in the senior quarters on Deck 2, 16 in officers' cabins on Deck 4 and 47 in the junior bunk rooms on Deck 4 (eight rooms of six berths, with one spare berth in Bunk 1).
