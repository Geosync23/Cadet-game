Step 2 in play: each lesson, step by step

Played Days 2-11 in a phone-sized window with a robot player that only follows the arrow, the job board and Tracked. For each lesson: what it is, how it opens, every step, what the game says today, whether it works; then the full chain of arrows from the invitation to "Trained", when the job is tracked. "Have" means today's arrow can already point there; "New" needs the queue, the panel pointer, or a fix first. The long form is plans/arrows-p5-step2-notes.md.

Rounds (Day 2, Torres)

Works but hiddenWalk 4-6 machines on one deck; look; mark Fine, Worn or Something's off. Opens Day 2 after the shadow shift; asked at 09:00 by toast and Mail ("Fancy a walk? ... (10:45, Works bay on Deck 5.)"), with no arrow if a job is tracked. Talk at the tea corner, then your first round (Deck 4, a washer first) is on the board; the arrow and a tip lead every step (the best-guided lesson in the game). Trained at turn-in ("Round clipboard is yours now"). Then a Morning round on the board each workday at 07:00, held to 11:00; from 5 rounds you pick the machines yourself. In play: works when you arrive with free hands. Holding a job when the talk ends, the arrow says "Take your first round" but the board won't give it; at 17:00 it lapses to the next morning, 06:00, before the shift, and the whole week slides a day.

The scanner and your first head-scratcher (Day 5, Emeka Halloran)

BrokenLook a faulty machine over: the scanner shows its parts with a bar and a word each; you pick the part. Opens Day 5 after one tuning (Torres is off on Fridays, so Emeka teaches). Asked at 06:00 ("Board, when you're in. Torres left you something. (09:00, Works bay on Deck 5.)"). In play: Emeka's demonstration job (Washer 4) was on the board for me; taking it trained me on the scanner without ever reading it. The head-scratcher on Washer 2 then finished as a plain repair, no case called; its clue toasts read "undefined". Afterwards, following the arrow ("Fetch a lamp" before any look-over) never uses the scanner, so it never counts, and Making it safe (Day 8) and the Tier 2 test never open. Going to the machine first, against the arrow, does open the scanner panel.

Also found in play (for the fix list)

  • A training that can't make its job (hauling off a Wednesday) should wait for the day it can and say so, not fail each morning.
  • A lesson that lapses comes back at 06:00, before the shift; it should come back after clock-in.
  • Dropping a job leaves its part in your hands; the next cart job refuses while the arrow points at the Cart bay. Needs a "Put the relay back: main storage" arrow.
  • A need arrow says "A shower, Deck 2" while its toast says Deck 4, and the Deck 2 spot has nothing to tap.
  • Tracked, with two things at the same time, can track the wrong one (Day 5: "On shift" instead of the scanner lesson); a training's Tracked arrow says only the room.
