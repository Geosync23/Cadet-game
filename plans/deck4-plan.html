What changes in Sketch 3
  • Every bunk room grows from 4 × 4 to 5 × 4, so it holds three ordinary bunk beds (6 berths) side by side, with the lockers by the door. 8 rooms × 6 = 48 berths: 47 juniors (the player included) and 1 empty. Today's rooms hold 4 each (32 berths).
  • Port side (Bunks 1–3): each room is one tile wider. The air scrubbers behind them shrink from 6 × 4 to 3 × 4, still sealed, still opening off the lobby.
  • Middle (Bunks 5–8, the night shift in Bunk 8): each room is one tile wider, taking the junior utility closet's 2 × 8 strip. That closet moves to the starboard side.
  • Starboard side (Bunk 4): one tile wider. The laundry keeps its size (4 × 4) and shifts one tile toward the lift. The junior stores shrink from 4 × 4 to 3 × 4. The utility closet becomes a 1 × 4 closet off the lobby, and the heating plant shrinks from 6 × 4 to 5 × 4.
  • Unchanged: both washrooms, the junior common room, the junior corridors, the lift lobby, all 24 officers' cabins (no guest cabins given up) and the music room.
  • For the build: Bunk 1 still holds the player's start tile; crew routes in the junior wing change with the walls. Berths are numbered 4-B<room>-1 to 6.
