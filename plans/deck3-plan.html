Deck 3: Commons

Sketch 1. Bow points forward, port side is on the left when facing it. Everyone comes here to eat. A wide promenade runs stern to bow down the middle of the ship, with the lift standing in a plaza halfway along: the garden at one end, the bow lounge at the other, and every room opening off it. Tap any room. Rooms ringed in gold are new ideas, not in the design doc yet.
Deck 3 at a glanceThe mess hall and galley (with a walk-in cold store) along the port side, the bow lounge with its bar filling the whole bow, the infirmary just aft of the lift, and the hydroponics garden across the stern. Washrooms off the plaza. Along the starboard side, three new ideas: a gym, a crew store and call-home booths. The water recycler and the garden's irrigation room sit sealed beside the garden.52 × 22 tiles, the widest deck: one row wider on each side than Deck 4, and a longer, rounder bow. The lift lines up with Decks 4 and 5. No freight lift: supplies come up the main lift on hover carts.

Utility rooms (built in 0.31.3)

Deck 3's three sealed service rooms get their insides, with nothing moved or resized: machines, pipes, a floor to stand and work on, and the hatch as the way in. Every machine has a machines entry (Pillar 2 plan, "Machines and jobs"), a panel tile beside it and a work:works place on that tile. Works crew and the player can pass the hatches; tier locks come later. Machine kinds are proposals for the systems engineer.
