Talking

People stay while you talk

Today: when you tap someone they stop for only 6 seconds, then walk back to their day, and the chat card closes when they're out of reach. That's why they walk away.

We'll build: they stay as long as the card is open (up to a minute if you leave it sitting there). They only go for something they can't miss: their shift in 10 minutes, bedtime, a tradition they're running. Then they say so ("Shift in five. Catch me later?").

Say hello and Talk a while

Say hello
once a day
+1 friendship, a little socialorTalk a while
once a day, a meter like eating
up to +3, plus up to +2 from your reply
  • Say hello is the old Chat, the quick nod in a corridor: +1 friendship (was +2) and a little social.
  • Talk a while takes 15 ship minutes (about 25 real seconds). You stand together, or sit down beside them if they're sitting. The bar fills social as you go (+30 for a full talk), and friendship goes up by 1 for every 5 minutes, up to +3. Lines from them come up every few seconds: their hobby, home, their day, ship news, a tradition coming up.
  • A choice partway through: three replies, one curious, one about you, one light. None is wrong: the one that suits them gives +2, the others +1.
  • So a good talk is about +5 against Say hello's +1 (a meal together is +3, a job together +6, a favour +8).
  • Walk off or tap Stop and you keep what you got.
  • Nobody turns you down for being new. They only say no if they really have to be somewhere.

New lines: a first batch of about 500: hellos for each friendship level, ten topics to talk about with openers and three-way replies, lines while you talk, "got to go" lines, and lines for each hobby. More come in a later pillar, as you said.

Built by a social engineer (new), lines by the head writer. The biggest job in this build; the story arcs are re-checked so they still reach their last beat.

  • Laundry duty is gone. No duty every two weeks, no junior laundry rota, no Day 9 duty, no laundry hand teaching you. Your own laundry is already built in 0.60.7, so Pillar 5's "uniform and laundry" shrinks to your uniform getting grubby on oily jobs and, later, servicing the washers.

  • The Thursday inspection and the officer ceremony check that you changed that morning.
  • The washer puzzles stay: Tuesday becomes the crew's big wash day.
  • Stores: Pillar 5's "hauling and stores" is partly done now (unloading the Wednesday delivery, the right shelf). Pillar 5 keeps the multi-stop delivery runs and the stocktake, which gets simpler because each shelf already shows its stock.
  • Leisure: side jobs are work and don't fill it; tinkering in the workshop does, a little.
  • Hobbies: tinkering in the workshop could become a seventh hobby when Pillar 5 builds it. Pillar 7's extra traditions can grow hobby circles out of Knit and natter.
