Deck 2: Command & Science

Sketch 5, for build 0.6.6. Bow points forward, port side is on the left when facing it. The deck splits at the lift. Every bedroom is aft, in the stern half (the left of this plan when it's wide): twelve for the senior crew and three for visitors, with the Captain's quarters across the stern. All science is forward, in the bow half. Step out of the lift's stern door and you're in the quarters; step out of its bow door and you're in science. A ring of lobby round the lift joins the two, with the Watch office looking over it. Tap any room. Rooms ringed in gold are new ideas, not in the design doc yet.

Open questions I had to guess at
  • Grown forward and wider, not aft. The deck is now 52 × 20 (sketch 4 was 50 × 18), the most it can be without outgrowing Deck 3. Growing aft would push the stern past Deck 3's stern, so it overhangs the deck below. Instead it grew two rows wider and two tiles forward. The stern half still holds fifteen bedrooms, one more than the brief, so the lift stays at [21, 10].
  • Six new senior crew. The design doc puts only the Captain, the XO and four Chiefs on Deck 2, so twelve bedrooms needs six more people: a Second Officer, a Senior Helm Officer, a Ship's Counsellor, a Comms Chief, a Deputy Survey Chief and a Senior Watch Officer (ringed in gold). If they move up from Deck 4, Deck 4 has 24 cabins for 16 officers. Are these the right six?
  • Fifteen bedrooms, not fourteen. The stern half had room for one more, so there's a second visitors' cabin as well as the visitors' suite and the Envoy's cabin. It could become a senior lounge instead.
  • Windows. The Captain and the eight hull cabins have windows. The six inner cabins between the corridors don't, like Deck 4's inner cabins; I gave them to the invented officers and the second visitors' cabin.
  • Room sizes. The Captain's quarters are 5 × 12 (60 tiles), the biggest room on the deck apart from the sensor array. The next biggest bedrooms are the two stern corner cabins, at 23 tiles. Hull cabins are 4 × 4 or 3 × 4, and inner cabins 4 × 4 or 3 × 4, so Deck 4's cabin template fits them.
  • The lift's two doors. The stern door opens onto the quarters lobby, which leads straight into both senior corridors. The bow door opens onto the science lobby and the science corridor. The lobby wraps round the lift above the trunks and below the ladder, so the two wings still join without going through the car. Should they be sealed apart instead?
  • Who lives where. Port hull, from the stern: Vance, the Works Chief, the Infirmary Chief, then Dr. Sato by the lift (two steps from science). Starboard hull: Envoy Saruth, Chief Holt next door (a story hook, or a bad idea?), the visitors' suite, then the Senior Helm Officer by the lift.
  • New science rooms. The chart room, comms room, specimen store and probe locker are new ideas. The probe locker is new in this sketch, filling the gap left when the Survey lab shrank so it stays smaller than the Captain's quarters.
  • Access. I've assumed the lobby, both corridor wings, the labs, the chart room, the comms room, the Watch office and the washroom are open from the start, and every bedroom is locked.
