Deck 2: Command & Science

Sketch 7, for build 0.6.6. Bow points forward, port side is on the left when facing it. The deck splits at the lift. Every bedroom is in the quarters, from the stern up to the lift (the left of this plan when it's wide): twelve for the senior crew and three for visitors. All science is forward of the lift. Step out of the lift's stern door and you're in the quarters, on a looped corridor: down the port side, across the gallery past the Captain's door, and back up the starboard side. Step out of the bow door and you're on the wide science corridor, which runs to a compact sensor array right in the nose. Tap any room. Rooms ringed in gold are new ideas, not in the design doc yet.
Deck 2 at a glanceThe quarters, from the stern to the lift: one looped senior corridor with fifteen bedrooms opening off it, twelve for the senior crew and three for visitors. The Captain's quarters fill the stern behind the loop's gallery, with windows all along the stern, and are the biggest room on the deck. Ten cabins line the hulls with windows, two of them beside the lift; four inner cabins sit inside the loop. Science, forward of the lift, along a wide science corridor: the Watch office and a washroom by the science lobby; the chart room, the Survey lab, the lab gas plant and a bow observatory to port; the comms room, the specimen store, the sample lab and a probe bay to starboard. Open questions I had to guess at
  • The array, chopped down. The sensor and comms array shrank from 238 tiles to 48, a sealed room in the very nose behind glass at the end of the science corridor, with a shut door beside the window. The Captain's quarters (60) are now the biggest room on the whole deck, array included.
  • What the freed bow became. Science got its width back. The chart room, Survey lab and comms room are 54 tiles each, the sample lab 56 and the specimen store 27, all at least their sketch 5 sizes. The science corridor is four tiles wide, like Deck 3's promenade. In the bow there are two new rooms: an observatory to port with windows round the bow (open, a place worth finding) and a probe bay to starboard (sealed). Each is 59 tiles, just under the Captain's 60. Too big? Either could shrink and give the array a little back.
  • Bedrooms couldn't grow further without crossing the lift. The quarters sit between the stern and the lift, both fixed, so the freed bow space can't reach them. Every bedroom is already at least its sketch 5 size, and most are bigger (sizes below). To make them bigger still, some cabins would have to move forward of the lift into the bow half, for example the two cabins beside the lift, widened forward. I've left that for you to decide.
  • Room sizes. Captain 60; stern corner cabins 29; other hull cabins 15–20; cabins inside the loop 16; Watch office 36.
  • As long as it can get. The deck now takes Deck 3's whole outline, so it can't grow any longer without overhanging the deck below. That's one tile longer than sketch 5, plus the two spare rows.
  • The loop. The senior corridor runs down the port side from the quarters lobby, across a short gallery at the stern end (past the Captain's door) and back up the starboard side to the lobby. The four inner cabins sit inside the loop. Is this the loop you had in mind? Sketches 2 and 3 had it right against the stern windows instead, but then the Captain loses the stern.
  • Captain's quarters. 5 × 12 (60 tiles) across the stern, with stern windows along its whole length. The biggest room on the deck.
  • Six new senior crew. The design doc puts only the Captain, the XO and four Chiefs on Deck 2, so twelve bedrooms needs six more people: a Second Officer, a Senior Helm Officer, a Ship's Counsellor, a Comms Chief, a Deputy Survey Chief and a Senior Watch Officer (ringed in gold). If they move up from Deck 4, Deck 4 has 24 cabins for 16 officers. Are these the right six?
  • Fifteen bedrooms. As in sketch 5, there's a second visitors' cabin as well as the visitors' suite and the Envoy's cabin. It could become a senior lounge instead.
  • Windows. The Captain and ten hull cabins have windows. Only the four inner cabins inside the loop don't, like Deck 4's inner cabins.
  • Who lives where. Port hull, from the stern: Vance, the Works Chief, the Infirmary Chief, Dr. Sato, then the Second Officer beside the lift. Starboard hull: Envoy Saruth, Chief Holt next door (a story hook, or a bad idea?), the visitors' suite, the Senior Helm Officer, then the Comms Chief beside the lift. Inside the loop: the Counsellor, the Senior Watch Officer, the Deputy Survey Chief and the second visitors' cabin.
  • The lift's two doors. The stern door opens onto the quarters lobby, where both ends of the loop meet. The bow door opens onto the science lobby, with the Watch office looking over it through glass. The lobby wraps round the lift above the trunks and below the ladder, so the two wings still join without going through the car.
  • Access. I've assumed the lobby, the loop, the labs, the chart room, the comms room, the Watch office and the washroom are open from the start, and every bedroom is locked.
